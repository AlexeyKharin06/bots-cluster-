- That single event accounts for 100% of MOVR's adverse signal

### Step 3: Outlier mechanism check
Looked at MOVR funding history on 4 exchanges around 2026-04-23 → 2026-04-27 in `multi_ex_funding_180.parquet`. The pattern:
- **2026-04-23 16:00 gate**: rate −1.18% (massive negative funding posting, the squeeze starts)
- Cross-venue: all 4 exchanges show elevated negative funding (binance trough −0.32%, bybit trough −0.39%, bitget trough −0.36%, gate trough −1.18%)
- This is a real **multi-venue funding squeeze event** — exactly what C2 is designed to detect
- The basis blew up because spot price rallied harder than perp during the squeeze (catalyst-driven retail buying chases spot, perp lags)

This is a **hedge-failure-during-volatile-rally** risk, not a venue or symbol-specific structural problem. It would affect ANY long-perp/short-spot trade on any symbol during similar conditions. Not a basis-of-blacklist.

## NEW: Methodology #35 (cross-venue blacklist gate)

**Promote to FINAL** (single corroboration sufficient because the rule is structural, not empirical):

> Before adding (sym, ex) pairs to a venue/symbol blacklist on cross-venue evidence, require **all three**:
> 1. **Raw n ≥ 5** events for the symbol across all venues (n=2-4 has no statistical resolution).
> 2. **All venues have negative mean** (cycle 0500 already used this).
> 3. **Trimmed mean (drop 1 worst event) remains negative** — eliminates single-outlier dominance.

INJ fails gate 1 (n=2). MOVR fails gate 3 (trimmed +0.35%). TAC passes all 3 (n=8, all venues neg, trimmed −0.62%).

Meth #35 is a **strengthening** of cycle 0500's implicit cross-venue rule, NOT a replacement. The 5-pair per-(ex,sym) blacklist (single-venue chronic issues like FLOW@binance) remains valid under its own rule (Meth #33 venue-Sharpe-gate applied per-venue).

## Companion finding: hedge-failure-on-rally risk

The MOVR 2026-04-24 outlier (+25% spot rally, basis broke −3.2%) is one of 29 events pool-wide with basis_drift < −3%. The full top-20 worst-basis-drift events include DRIFT×6, FLOW×6, RAVE, SIREN, PIPPIN, BLUR — all already known thin small-caps that have been on watch/blacklist via Meth #33. The mechanism is consistent: **when a small-cap rallies 20%+ on news-catalyst, perp/spot basis temporarily inverts in the direction that hurts long-perp/short-spot.** None of these need to be added to extended blacklist on the basis of one event each — the cycle 2300 5-pair already covers the chronic offenders.

Potential **Meth #36 candidate** (not promoted, just filed): impose a per-event **realized-vol gate** — if symbol's 1h or 4h price std at trigger exceeds the 95th percentile of its 30d distribution, **DEFER entry by 12h** until vol normalizes. This would have skipped MOVR 2026-04-24 (entry during a vertical rally). Cost: throughput, requires 30d rolling vol per (sym, ex) recomputed at trigger.
