## Part 3 — Mechanism: why perp-perp hedge zeros out the divergence signal

### 3.1 — What divergence actually measures

`c2.div = max(rates) − min(rates)` across exchanges in a 1h bucket. When this is ≥100bp, there is a large funding-rate dispersion across venues for that symbol at that hour.

Two mechanistic possibilities for what a high-divergence event represents (per cycle 1247 Meth #22 candidate writeup):

**Hypothesis A (noise / outlier marker):** One exchange has an anomalous rate (delayed update, illiquid quote, broken oracle). The "outlier" exchange is unrepresentative of true funding. If your trade pivots on the outlier ex, basis hedge is mispriced.

**Hypothesis B (systemic stress amplifier):** All exchanges are repricing rapidly under broad market stress; divergence measures the speed of repricing. The longer the basis stays stretched, the bigger the PnL on hedged capture.

Cycle 1247 (H31, basis) supported A (negative filter, n=116). Cycle 1700 (H38, basis) supported B (positive filter, n=10,686).

### 3.2 — Why perp-perp hedge changes the calculus

Basis hedge = SHORT spot. PnL = primary perp funding accrual (event-determined) − basis convergence cost. Divergence is exogenous noise on the funding read.

Perp-perp hedge = SHORT perp on a different exchange (in H34's case, deterministic fixed_binance_else_bybit). PnL = primary perp funding + hedge perp funding. The hedge perp leg is itself one of the venues whose funding-rate dispersion `div` is measuring. **If the hedge exchange is the one with the off-side rate, the divergence directly feeds into the hedge's funding contribution.**

Result: the negative/positive divergence effect that basis hedge externalizes (as a PnL surprise on the spot leg) is internalized as part of the hedge leg PnL in perp-perp. The "outlier noise" gets absorbed: if the hedge ex is the high-funding outlier, the SHORT hedge collects more positive funding (PnL boost); if the hedge ex is the low-funding outlier, the SHORT hedge collects less (PnL drag). Symmetric absorption → average zero contribution → null filter effect.

This is consistent with:
- 3/5 per-ex slight-negative (likely tail of Hypothesis A bleeding through when hedge ex is NOT the outlier ex)
- 2/5 per-ex slight-positive (likely tail of Hypothesis B when hedge ex IS the outlier ex, capturing stress payment)
- Net: averages to zero

### 3.3 — Predicted fourth cell

The untested cell is **magnitude trigger + perp-perp hedge**. Under the absorption hypothesis, perp-perp should also flatten H38's positive filter. Predicted: **NULL or weakly POSITIVE** (because H38's universe-wide stress is real, but the hedge ex is symmetric about it).
