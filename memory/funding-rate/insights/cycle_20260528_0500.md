## Interpretation

The two R13 feature components fail to transfer for two distinct, mutually reinforcing reasons:

1. **Sign-flip is a structural impossibility in the validated domain.** R13's failure was *defined by* the funding reversing direction. The validated edge selects exactly the `pre<0` events where funding persists — by construction the reversal never happens (0/101). A filter built on "exclude reversals" is vacuous: it can never fire on the validated stream. The feature was not just "weak" — it was diagnostic of a population that the validated filter already excludes.

2. **The magnitude proxy is meaning-inverted, hence null as a filter.** In R13, larger |pre_rate| → larger flip → worse. In H34 the same magnitude is uncorrelated with PnL (r=0.032) because the perp-perp hedge neutralizes price/regime variance and the funding capture is structurally locked. Importing "high magnitude = danger" therefore strips throughput (−25%) and Sharpe (−0.06) while moving mean by −0.005pp. The naive transfer is strictly dominated by doing nothing — confirmed in BOTH walk-forward halves.

This is the textbook Meth #26 outcome: **a feature's predictive meaning is variant-specific; the only legitimate way to use R13's insight on H34 would be to RE-DERIVE a feature from H34's own structure** (which is what H_COMBO_3c did, and it worked). Direct transfer of the rejected variant's discriminator carries zero signal.
