Sanity checks:

- c2 ts_bucket range covers H31 ts range (h31: 2025-11-06 → 2026-04-25 vs c2: 2025-11-02 → 2026-04-30)

### Method — classification

For each H31 LONG event at (exchange E, symbol S, timestamp T):

1. **C2 PRE-confirm**: Look up c2 rows at (S, T-4h), (S, T-3h), (S, T-2h), (S, T-1h). If any has div≥50bp → `c2_pre50=True`.
2. **C2 COINCIDENT**: Look up c2 row at (S, T). If div≥50bp → `c2_coin50=True`.
3. **C2_ANY at threshold X**: `c2_anyX = c2_preX | c2_coinX` evaluated for X∈{30, 50, 100} bp.
4. **Meth #17 CONFIRMED**: At (S, T), count OTHER exchanges (excluding E) with rate ≤ -50bp. If ≥1 → `meth17=True`.
5. **Max divergence in window**: `max_div_5h` = max c2.div over (S, T-4h..T).

Hourly-bucket alignment uses `ts // 3_600_000 * 3_600_000`, which matches c2_wide's own bucketing.

## Part 3 — Interpretation and methodology

### 3.1 — Why the 100bp divergence INVERSION makes mechanical sense

Cross-ex DIVERGENCE (max-min spread) measures DISAGREEMENT, not stress level. Two scenarios produce ≥100bp divergence:

a) **All exchanges stressed but at different magnitudes** (e.g., Binance -200bp, Bybit -300bp, Gate -100bp). Here, divergence = 200bp BUT all venues confirm same-direction stress. Meth #17 also fires. Good for hedged trade.

b) **One exchange diverges, others quiet or anti-aligned** (e.g., Bybit -150bp, others -10bp to +20bp). Divergence = 170bp, BUT Meth #17 does NOT fire (no other ex ≤ -50bp). This is an idiosyncratic stress.

The 2x2 cross-tab shows:
- (b)-type events at 100bp threshold (meth17=False ∧ c2_100=True, n=6): mean +2.20%
- (a)-type events at 100bp threshold (meth17=True ∧ c2_100=True, n=22): mean +2.87%

Both (a) and (b) underperform the "non-divergent" tier. This is the **key insight**:

> Even systemic stress (a) suffers when there's substantial cross-ex disagreement. The basis convergence is path-dependent on the OUTLIER venue's funding curve — and outliers can stay outliers longer than the 4-hour trade window.

So the c2_div_100bp filter is **NOT a Meth #17 substitute** — it's an orthogonal quality screener. Combine the two for cleanest trades:
- Meth #17 selects: stress is SYSTEMIC (good)
- ¬c2_100 selects: stress is ALIGNED across venues (clean basis path)
- COMBO = systemic AND aligned = optimal hedged-basis setup

### 3.2 — METHODOLOGY #22 CANDIDATE — divergence as a NEGATIVE quality filter on hedged trades

**Statement:**

> For hedged funding-capture trades on a primary exchange, cross-venue MAX-MIN DIVERGENCE (the size of the largest spread between any two exchanges' funding rates at the same instant) is a NEGATIVE quality signal at strict thresholds (≥100bp). Combined with Meth #17 CONFIRMED (same-direction stress signal), divergence identifies events where the basis path is OUTLIER-DRIVEN rather than systemic-stress-driven.

**Direction (vs Meth #14/#17 sign-flip rule):**

| Trade structure | Meth #14/#17 sign | Meth #22 sign | Why different |
|---|---|---|---|
| HEDGED funding-capture | CONFIRMED > SOLO (positive filter) | Low DIVERGENCE > High DIVERGENCE (negative filter at extremes) | Meth17 ≠ ¬Div — Meth17 tests SAME-DIRECTION confirmation; Div tests CROSS-EX SPREAD. They overlap but are not equivalent. |
| UNHEDGED mean-rev | SOLO > CONFIRMED (negative filter) | Not yet tested | TBD; H3-like trades would need separate test. |

**Provenance for Meth #22 candidate:**
- H31 LONG-only n=116 (this cycle): 100bp threshold gap -1.04pp (consistent across walk-fwd TRAIN -1.43 / TEST -0.58)
- COMBO (meth17 ∧ ¬c2_100) gap vs baseline: +0.43pp mean / Sharpe lift +0.20 stable across walk-fwd

**Promotion criteria for Meth #22 → confirmed:**
- Cross-validate on H38 hedged (n=10,686 — cycle 2300 data) with cross-tab Meth17 × c2_div_100bp. Would need same hourly bucketing applied across that dataset. ~30 min compute. Filed for next cycle.
- Cross-validate on H34 perp-perp hedge (n=101) — smaller sample, less decisive.

If Meth #22 holds on H38, it would be promoted from candidate to confirmed and become a standard filter on all hedged backtests going forward.

## Part 8 — Caveats and limitations

1. **n=70 for COMBO filter is small for Sharpe claims.** Sharpe 2.04 has high CI given small sample. The robust statistic is the **walk-fwd directional consistency** (both halves +1.1pp gap vs excluded subset) — this is what makes the finding non-noise.

2. **Meth #17 on H31 (n=92 CONFIRMED, n=24 SOLO) is small-n echo of cycle 2300's H38 (n=10,686) finding.** The H31 gap +0.87pp could be noise on its own; only consistent because of decisive H38 evidence already in hand.

3. **c2_wide does not cover Bitget for some symbols** (n_ex<2 for some H31 events). Where the event has only 1 ex covered in c2 (limited cross-ex data), divergence is NaN — counted as "no divergence" in this analysis. ~5-10 events affected; unlikely to flip findings.

4. **Hourly bucketing may miss intra-hour C2 divergence spikes** that resolve before the bucket boundary. c2_wide IS hourly — finer grain would require re-fetch. Acceptable for this initial COMBO test.

5. **The COMBO filter sacrifices 40% throughput.** At 12 events/month vs 19/month baseline, smaller sample for paper validation. Trade-off must be approved by user.

6. **H_COMBO_1 reject vs accept depends on framing.** If "H_COMBO_1" means "naive confluence super-edge" → REJECTED. If it means "any meaningful combination of H31 and C2 signals" → REFINED into H31_QUALITY_COMBO. Cycle log uses the literal BRIEF framing (rejected) and files the refinement separately.
