## Operational implication

H31_BASIS_PAPER target** — these can be executed without any cross-venue transfer.
The other 53 are H34 candidates. Cross-venue spot transfers (10 events) are
operationally complex (deposit lead time, withdrawal fees, idle capital) and probably
should be DROPPED in the paper-stream design unless mean edge justifies the complexity.

## Bybit borrow-rate sanity check

Pulled current Bybit hourlyBorrowRate from same endpoint. For our 25 Bybit-primary
symbol set, only 10 (40%) have Bybit margin available. For those 10:

| stat | value |
|---|---|
| Median daily borrow | 5bp/day |
| p25 | 3bp/day |
| p75 | 13bp/day |
| Max | 67bp/day |

The cycle_2300 basis-hedge PnL model assumed 30bp/day fallback for missing-borrow
symbols. Real median is **6× LOWER** (5 vs 30bp/day). At 4h hold, default cost was
5bp; real is ~0.8bp. The basis-hedge net edge is therefore UNDERSTATED by ~4bp/event
in prior reports. Doesn't change conclusion (effect is in our favor); just confirms
the result is conservatively reported.

Worst-case: at max 67bp/day → 11bp per 4h, still well within the algebraic structural
WR (min funding event +80bp > 11bp + 20bp slip = 31bp worst-case cost).

## Caveats / honest limitations

1. **Live snapshot only.** The margin universes are CURRENT snapshots (2026-05-22).
   Margin pair lists evolve — symbols get added/removed, borrow rates change.
   Backtest treats them as static across the 180-day window. For events from 2025-11
   this is wrong; e.g., a symbol delisted from Bybit margin in 2026-02 would not
   appear in the current snapshot. Direction of bias: under-counts historical coverage
   (delistings) but probably few in our 6-month window.

2. **isBorrowable flag only.** We treated "appears in margin list" as "SHORT-spot
   feasible". In practice each venue also has a daily-borrow quota limit; if the pool
   is exhausted, you can't borrow even if listed. Production must check live quota at
   trade time. None of our 6-month backtest data could check this — out of scope.

3. **Quote currency.** We restricted to USDT pairs. Some symbols may be margin-listable
   only on USDC quote (rare). Didn't check — likely <2% impact.

4. **Bybit borrow rate from VIP-0 tier.** Real rate for a higher-tier account is lower.
   30bp/day proxy was probably already conservative; real-world account at VIP-1+
   would see further savings.

5. **No live transfer-cost data on cross-venue spot route.** Used 10bp placeholder;
   real-world withdrawal fees vary 5-50bp depending on symbol/network. Probably
   makes cross-venue spot route LESS attractive than +2.95% suggests.
