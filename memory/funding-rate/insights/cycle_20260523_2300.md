# Cycle 20260523_2300 — H3-FU-3 multi-venue coincidence: HYPOTHESIS INVERTED, FILTER ACCEPTED

## TL;DR

H3-FU-3 multi-venue coincidence filter executed on the 129-event vol-vetted Edge-3 paper-stream universe. **The expected mechanism (per BRIEF predicting "multi-venue filter drops Binance-TUSD-only losses") was INVERTED by data**: solo-venue depegs are the FAST-reverting tier (median 2h hold, 100% WR), multi-venue CONFIRMED depegs are the SLOW-reverting tier (median 112h hold, 59% reached_peg, 3 of 3 historical losses concentrated here). **Net result**: DROP-CONFIRMED filter still **accepted** — it lifts mean PnL +10% (+0.805%→+0.889%), WR +1.9pp (96.1%→98.0%), drops 3/3 known losses, walk-fwd asymmetric PASS (Meth #12). Cost: throughput −22% (129→101 events), KuCoin USDe/USDD concentration 73.6%→91.1%. New **Methodology #14**: multi-venue coincidence is a **SYSTEMIC-DEPEG MARKER, not a NOISE-DEPEG MARKER** — invert the intuition for all future cross-venue-confirmation filters.

## Setup

- **Universe**: 129 events in `/tmp/h3_fu6_paper.parquet` (50bp tier post-Meth #13 vol-vet).
- **Cross-venue discovery** (`/tmp/h3_fu3_discover.py`): polled Binance/Bybit/OKX/KuCoin/Gate/Bitget/MEXC spot symbols for {USDC,TUSD,USDP,FDUSD,USDD,USDE}USDT listings. Result: every headline stable has ≥2 other venues. But effective historical coverage is sparser — Gate/MEXC/Bitget all have 35-day lookback caps (known limit from H3-A3 cycle), reducing viable cross-venues for older events to Binance/Bybit/KuCoin/OKX (latter only for USDC).
- **Cross-venue OHLCV fetch** (`/tmp/h3_fu3_fetch.py`): for each event × each other venue listing the stable, fetched ±2h of 5m bars. 381 (event, other_venue) attempts → 5321 bars across ~110 successful fetches. 271 fetches returned empty (mostly Gate/MEXC/Bitget on pre-35d events).
- **Coincidence scoring** (`/tmp/h3_fu3_coincidence.py`):
  - `coincident_30`: count of other venues with **same-direction** dev ≥30bp anywhere in **±30min** of entry_ts
  - `coincident_60`: same, ±60min
  - **Categorize**:
    - `UNLISTED` — no other venue lists this stable (n=0 — never happens; all 6 have ≥2 venues)
    - `UNVERIFIABLE` — listed elsewhere but 0 viable bars (historical lookback miss) → n=65
    - `CONFIRMED` — ≥1 other venue ≥30bp same-dir within ±30min → n=27
    - `CONFIRMED_LOOSE` — only ±60min confirms, not ±30min → n=1
    - `SOLO` — viable cross-venue exists but no concurrent depeg ≥30bp → n=36

## Mechanism confirmation — hold-time decisively explains the flip

```
                  mean_hold  median  reached_peg
CONFIRMED          85.2 h    112 h    59%
CONFIRMED_LOOSE    87.9 h     88 h   100%
SOLO                9.2 h      2 h   100%
UNVERIFIABLE       42.2 h     17 h    89%
```

- SOLO depegs **mean-revert in median 2 hours, 100% reach peg** → exit on the 10bp peg condition fast, low time-in-market, no max-hold timeouts.
- CONFIRMED depegs take **median 112 HOURS (~5 days), 41% never reach peg** → these are real market-wide imbalances that the 7d max-hold sometimes cannot resolve. Three of those 27 CONFIRMED events hit max-hold at a loss (the entire historical loss count of the 50bp tier).

This is exactly the inverse of what the BRIEF predicted but the mechanism is consistent: a real multi-venue depeg = real systemic pressure (e.g. Tether-fear cycle pulling all stables in same direction) → mean reversion is delayed because the cause persists. A solo-venue depeg = idiosyncratic local-orderbook imbalance → reverts within hours because no broader force sustains it.

## Decision: ACCEPT filter as OPTIONAL paper-stream variant, NOT replace baseline

Two paper-stream specs (both should run in parallel):

**A) H3_DEPEG_PAPER_FULL** (baseline, unchanged)
- Universe: G_paper 50bp / 75bp on {USDC,USDP,FDUSD,TUSD,USDD,USDE,PYUSD} × {Binance, KuCoin, Bybit-PYUSD}
- Trigger: |spot−$1|≥75 (primary tier) or 50 (wider) + 12h cooldown + NOT phantom_print + notional gate
- Exit: ±10bp OR 24h. 10bp/leg slip.
- Headline: 50bp n=129 +0.81%/WR 96.1%; 75bp n=39 +1.76%/WR 100%
- Diversification: KuCoin 73.6%

**B) H3_DEPEG_PAPER_SOLOFLAG** (new, layered)
- Same trigger; at entry-time, also fetch cross-venue OHLCV ±30min from up-to-3 other CEXes (Bybit/KuCoin/Binance/Gate-if-recent).
- If `coincident_30 ≥ 1` (any other venue ≥30bp same direction) → **TAG_CONFIRMED, half-size or skip per risk budget**.
- Else (SOLO or UNVERIFIABLE) → **full-size**.
- Filtered headline: 50bp n=101 +0.89%/WR 98%; 75bp n=30 +1.96%/WR 100%
- Diversification: KuCoin 91.1% (worse)

Recommend run B as **paper variant alongside A** for ~30 days. If live data confirms median-hold gap (CONFIRMED slow, SOLO fast), the filter's value becomes **execution-risk reduction** (less capital tied up in slow-reverting positions) rather than just a PnL lift.

## Methodology lesson #14 — invert the cross-venue intuition

> **For DEPEG arb, multi-venue coincidence = SYSTEMIC stress = SLOW revert.
> Single-venue depeg = IDIOSYNCRATIC noise = FAST revert + structural reversion.
> Apply the filter INVERSELY to instinct: CONFIRMED is the harder regime.**

Generalizable to any "follow-the-confirmation" filter on mean-reverting strategies. Confirmation = sustained driver, not validation of the signal. Lesson #14 added to methodology canon. Retroactive note: this contradicts H3-A3's offhand finding that "PYUSD multi-venue would discard 90% of events" — that observation is consistent with this lesson, since the discarded events would have included the highest-PnL events.
