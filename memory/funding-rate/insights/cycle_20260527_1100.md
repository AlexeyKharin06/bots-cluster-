## 4. METHODOLOGY #28 CANDIDATE — HOLD-TIME-TAIL DRAG

### Statement
For any scalp strategy with a fixed maximum hold (timeout), the trade distribution typically bimodalizes:
- A "fast print" mode (trades close at TARGET well before timeout) — usually profitable, often very high WR
- A "stale" mode (trades that didn't print and run out clock at timeout) — usually unprofitable, often catastrophic
Aggregate stats average these two distributions and undersell BOTH: a real edge in the fast-print mode AND a real anti-edge in the stale mode coexist in one bot.

### Implications
- Reporting only `mean ROI / WR / sum_PnL` over all trades hides the bimodal structure
- The "edge" can often be **isolated by hold-time filter** (or equivalently, by a short-circuit early-exit rule)
- The "anti-edge" in the stale mode is structural: by the time the timeout fires, the price has already moved adversely on average (because trades that move favorably exit early via TARGET; the surviving trades are the bad ones — classic selection)

### Promotion path
Need 2 corroborations to promote to CONFIRMED methodology:
1. ✅ **paper_fairprice_v6** (this cycle): sub-60s WR 97% +0.50%, ≥60s WR 50% −0.46%

   - paper_new_symbol (n=13 — too small currently but stratifiable: trades have hold_sec field)
   - H38_CONFIRMED operational tier (n=5324) — has exit timestamps; could stratify by realized hold duration
   - Historical mega_fairprice grid {hold_min, exit_reason} cross-tab — clearly visible already (TARGET mean +0.279 vs TIMEOUT mean −1.708 in raw stats)
