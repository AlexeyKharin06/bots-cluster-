## T2 classifier — design

### Priority-ordered ruleset (first match wins)

1. **O_depeg** — coin in `{USDD,USDE,USDP,TUSD,FDUSD,USDC,DAI,GUSD,PYUSD,PAX,UST,USDX}` OR text matches `(депе|depeg|usdd|usde|fdusd|tusd|usdp|peg)`.
2. **O_hack** — text matches `(взлом|hack|exploit|эксплойт|drained?)`.
3. **FB** (high-confidence) — `interval_changed_in_window=True` AND `max_caps ≥ 5` AND `max_fund ≥ 1.0%`. This is the textbook H31 footprint.
4. **FB** (med-confidence) — `interval_changed_in_window=True` AND `max_fund ≥ 0.3%`. Interval-shortening alone implies stress.
5. **F** (high) — `max_caps ≥ 8` AND `max_fund ≥ 0.5%` AND no interval change. Pure funding capture (cap-hitting without shortening = chronic but not-yet-shortened).
6. **F** (med) — `max_caps ≥ 3` AND `max_fund ≥ 0.5%`.
7. **B** (med/low) — `spread_hits ≥ 1-2` in text AND no funding-related text AND `max_caps < 3`. Pure basis claim, no funding signature.
8. **L** — text matches `(листинг|listing|new\s+token|fresh\s+listing)`.
9. **F** (low) — funding text + ≥1 cap hit, residual.
10. **FB** (low) — both spread AND funding mentioned + caps + no shortening.
11. **O_news** — news keyword.
12. **O** (default) — no clear match.

### Confidence layering

- `high`: hard-feature anchor (interval change + caps, hack/depeg keyword, etc.)
- `med`: 1 strong feature OR 1 strong keyword
- `low`: text-only, ambiguous

Two issues:
1. **Two-word phrase requirement**: `\bfunding\b.{0,5}\brate\b` requires the literal phrase "funding rate" with at most 5 chars between. The lone keyword "funding" (which DOES appear in many RU-translit-friendly posts like "funding bingx") does NOT match.

**Patch (proposed, requires user OK before applying — touches `.shared/` infra used by all 4 projects)**:

```python
'funding-rate': [
    r'\bfunding\b', r'\bperp\b', r'\bperpetual\b', r'\boi\b',
    r'\bopen interest\b', r'\bbasis\b', r'\bspread\b', r'\bhedge\b',
    r'фанд', r'спред', r'хедж', r'перп', r'интервал', r'арбитраж',
    r'фьюч', r'премиум', r'премія', r'фьючи',
],
```

**Validation plan post-patch**:
1. Re-process last 24h of `signals_master.jsonl` through the patched filter; check ratio (expect 5-15%).

## Methodology lesson (CANDIDATE) #18 — Mechanism-stratified universe coverage

> When validating a strategy via cross-reference with practitioner reports, measure **coverage of practitioner-reported events**, not just precision/recall of detected events. A detector with high mean-PnL on its own detections may still be missing 60-70% of the addressable opportunity set if its symbol universe is narrower than the practitioner universe. Coverage gap is an explicit quantity: `n_practitioner_events_matched / n_practitioner_events_with_same_mechanism`. Report this alongside Sharpe.
>
> Applied to H31: 3/10 = 30% practitioner-FB coverage with current 50-symbol universe. The 70% miss is universe-bound, not mechanism-misspecification. Two failure modes are distinct:
> - **Mechanism miss** (we don't have the right trigger criteria) → fix detector, expect more events on existing symbols.
> - **Universe miss** (mechanism is right but symbols absent) → fix universe, expect more events on more symbols.
>
> H31's miss is the second kind. The fix is symbol expansion (R4-risky), not detector change.

Filed as candidate Meth #18. Will graduate to canon if a second strategy shows the same pattern.
