## Part 1 — Data inputs and method

### Inputs

```

  cols used: exchange, sym_norm, ts, ts_bucket, net_basis_conservative,
             cat_50bp (∈ {SOLO, CONFIRMED}), abs_rate, mag_bucket

/tmp/c2_wide.parquet                1,092,384 rows × 10 cols
  cols used: sym_norm, ts_bucket, div (max-min spread across exchanges)
```

### Method

For each H38 event at (exchange E, symbol S, ts_bucket B):
1. Build dict lookup `c2_by_sym[S][B] → div` from c2_wide.
2. For each event, compute `c2_max_div = max(c2_by_sym[S][B + k*1h])` over `k ∈ {-4, -3, -2, -1, 0}` (4-hour pre-event window inclusive of event hour).
3. Threshold flags: `c2_30bp`, `c2_50bp`, `c2_100bp` = `c2_max_div ≥ thr`.
4. Cross-tab `cat_50bp × c2_100bp` on `net_basis_conservative` PnL.
5. Walk-forward 50/50 by `ts`.

### Coverage check

`c2_max_div` coverage: 9,196 / 10,686 = **86.1%** of H38 events have at least one c2 bucket within window. NaN events treated as `c2_100bp=False` (conservative; would only dampen positive signal if anything).

## Part 3 — Why does Meth #22 sign FLIP between H31 and H38?

### 3.1 — Mechanistic difference

**H31 trigger** = exchange E performs an OFFICIAL ACTION (shortens funding interval e.g. 4h→1h). This action is the trigger; it implies E has decided this symbol is in stress.

**H38 trigger** = exchange E has |funding| ≥ 30bp on a single hourly bar. This is a magnitude observation; no behavioral inference.

When `c2_100bp=True` at the trigger hour:

- **H31 case**: "exchange E shortened interval, AND there's ≥100bp cross-ex spread on this sym."
  - Most likely scenario: E is the OUTLIER. Other exchanges have very different funding rates (because they didn't shorten the interval — their funding curves haven't repriced).
  - Hedging E with the spot leg or another exchange's perp: the OTHER side hasn't re-priced. Basis convergence is path-dependent on whether OTHER catches up (slow) or E reverts (fast).
  - Outcome: noisier 4h basis-hedged PnL. NEGATIVE filter.

- **H38 case**: "exchange E has |rate|≥30bp on a single bar, AND there's ≥100bp cross-ex spread."
  - Most likely scenario: the symbol is GLOBALLY stressed; multiple exchanges have wide rates, the universe is dislocated.
  - High divergence ≥100bp suggests big basis-spread opportunity (in fact, the wider the divergence, the bigger the cross-ex arbitrage).
  - Hedging on the higher-magnitude leg captures more total basis PnL.
  - Outcome: bigger hedged-basis PnL. POSITIVE filter.

### 3.2 — Why H38 SOLO ∧ c2_100bp=True n=342 is the weakest "True" cell

```
SOLO ∧ c2_100bp=True: n=342 mean=+1.851%
```

This cell has ≥100bp divergence BUT no other exchange same-direction confirmation. Meaning the only stressed exchange is E; others may be neutral or anti-aligned. The "high divergence" then represents IDIOSYNCRATIC stress on E.

Compare to `CONFIRMED ∧ c2_100bp=True`: n=1554 mean=+2.838%. Here, ≥100bp divergence AND other exchanges confirm same direction. This is SYSTEMIC stress with wide range — best of both worlds.

The cross-tab reveals the underlying signal isn't divergence alone, but **divergence as a multiplier on confirmed systemic stress**.

### 3.3 — Why the H31 cycle 1247 finding survives within its narrow scope

H31 events have n=116 — small sample. The H31-LONG-only filter ALREADY selects for `pre_rate<0` (basis-favorable starting point). Within this narrow universe, the small subgroup of n=28 with c2_any100=True has a different "type" composition than H38's 1896-event sample — likely dominated by outlier-divergence cases where the H31 trigger exchange is anomalous.

The cycle 1247 H31_QUALITY_COMBO finding remains LOCALLY valid (within H31's interval-shortening universe), but **cannot be extrapolated** to:
- H38's broader magnitude-trigger universe (this cycle: opposite sign)
- H34's perp-perp hedge universe (untested, but predicted to align with H38 since H34 is also magnitude-triggered)
- H3's depeg universe (untested, structurally different mean-reversion mechanism)

## Part 4 — METHODOLOGY #23 candidate (filed)

**Statement:**

> Cross-venue DIVERGENCE (max-min funding spread) as a quality filter on hedged trades is **trigger-conditional**:
> - When the strategy trigger is **exchange-specific behavioral** (e.g., interval-shortening: ex E performs an action), divergence ≥100bp marks E as the OUTLIER. Basis convergence becomes outlier-path-dependent. **NEGATIVE filter.**
> - When the strategy trigger is **universe-wide quantitative** (e.g., magnitude: any ex has |rate|≥threshold), divergence ≥100bp marks systemic stress amplitude. **POSITIVE filter.**
>
> Practical consequence: cycle 1247 H31_QUALITY_COMBO finding does NOT generalize. Each hedged-trade family must independently test divergence sign on its own trigger universe.

**Provenance:**
- H31 LONG-only n=116 (cycle 1247): c2_100bp=True gap −1.04pp
- H38 |rate|≥30bp n=10,686 (this cycle): c2_100bp=True gap +1.19pp
- Walk-fwd both halves preserve respective signs on respective universes

**Subsumes Meth #22 candidate as a special case.** Meth #22 was "divergence is universally a negative filter on hedged trades"; promoted to "trigger-conditional" rule.

**Promotion criteria for Meth #23 → confirmed:**
- Replicate on one additional hedged-trade family (e.g., H34 perp-perp hedge n=101). Predict POSITIVE filter (H34 is magnitude-conditioned, not behavioral). ~20 min next cycle.
