## Result 1 — Pendle Boros YU instrument (NEW EDGE CANDIDATE)

### Instrument mechanics

YU = Yield Unit. One YU represents one notional unit of a perpetual market's funding stream until maturity (e.g. 1 BTC of Binance BTC-PERP funding). Two prices:

- **Implied APR**: fixed rate locked at YU entry
- **Underlying APR**: floating funding rate received/paid by perp longs/shorts on that venue

For a YU LONG: receive Underlying APR (float), pay Implied APR (fixed). For YU SHORT: pay float, receive fixed. The YU is essentially a per-venue funding-rate interest-rate swap.

### Cross-venue spread arbitrage construction (the alpha)

4-leg synthetic that captures the FIXED rate spread between two venues' funding rates:

| Leg | Action | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | SHORT Hyperliquid-YU | pay HL underlying float, receive HL implied fixed |
| 2 | SHORT Hyperliquid BTC perp | pay funding (= HL underlying float to longs) |
| 3 | LONG Binance-YU | receive Binance underlying float, pay Binance implied fixed |
| 4 | LONG Binance BTC perp | receive funding (= Binance underlying float) |

Floating components cancel pairwise (leg 1's HL float vs leg 2's HL float; leg 3's Binance float vs leg 4's Binance float). What remains: **+HL Implied APR − Binance Implied APR = fixed spread**.

Delta exposure: SHORT HL perp + LONG Binance perp on same notional → zero BTC price exposure (provided perp prices stay coupled, which they do at <30bp basis structurally).

### Documented edge magnitude (Pendle's own backtest, Oct-Nov 2025)

| Market | Maturity | Avg Fixed APR | Peak APR |
|---|---|---|---|
| BTC | 31 Oct 2025 | 11.4% | 23.5% |
| BTC | 28 Nov 2025 | 6.42% | 12.36% |
| ETH | 31 Oct 2025 | 9.94% | 23.88% |
| ETH | 28 Nov 2025 | 5.98% | 17.1% |

"Prior instances reaching as high as 48% APR" mentioned (rare).

### Costs (per round-trip, not annualized)

- Boros: 0.05% on position value + 0.2% flat on settlement
- Binance perp: 0.02% maker / 0.05% taker
- Hyperliquid perp: 0.015% maker / 0.045% taker
- Slippage: "0.2% of the YU orderbook consumed per trade"
- Total round-trip fee budget ≈ 50–80bp per cycle

→ Net APR ≈ (gross APR) − (fees per cycle × cycles per year). For a 30-day maturity at 11.4% gross APR: gross = 11.4% × 30/365 = 94bp; minus ~70bp fees = ~24bp net per cycle. Net annualized ≈ 24bp × 12 = 2.9% if held for full maturity once; higher if entered/exited at peak APR windows.

### Risks

1. **wBTC counterparty** — Boros uses wBTC collateral for BTC market. Adds BitGo solvency tail risk.
2. **Liquidation** — each of 4 legs has independent liquidation conditions (perp legs liquidate on directional move; YU legs liquidate on Implied APR swings)
3. **Liquidity** — max trade size at peak APR "low-6 figures in margin". OK for paper validation; capping for live.
4. **Maturity** — YU has fixed expiry; ladder management required for continuous exposure
5. **Arbitrum infrastructure** — we don't have execution infra on Arbitrum/EVM yet
6. **Smart contract risk** — Boros is a 1-year-old protocol on Arbitrum

### Why this is NEW (vs our existing edges)

| Edge | Mechanism axis | Trigger | Hold | Capital efficiency |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| H31 basis-hedge | per-exchange funding magnitude | interval-shortening event | 4h–16h | high (event-driven) |
| H34 perp-perp | cross-exchange funding *level* gap | same as H31 | 4h–16h | high |
| H3 depeg | stablecoin price reversion | spot-vs-$1 deviation | 2h–7d | medium |
| **H_BOROS_YU_ARB (NEW)** | **cross-venue funding-rate FIXED spread** | **YU implied APR ≠ realized funding gap** | **0–30d to maturity** | **medium-low (capital-locked across 4 legs)** |

The key novelty: Boros lets you trade the **funding-rate SPREAD as a fixed instrument** rather than collecting it as a variable accrual. The mechanism is closer to an interest-rate-swap basis trade than a funding-capture trade. This is **orthogonal in instrument class** even if the underlying signal (funding-rate gap) overlaps with H34.

Predicted correlation to H31/H34/H3:
- vs H31: low (different trigger — Boros pricing inefficiency vs interval-change event)
- vs H34: moderate-to-high (same underlying gap, but H34 collects the gap variably as it varies; Boros locks the gap as it's currently priced. The two are time-shifted views of the same phenomenon — if H34 is profitable, Boros YU buying-the-floating-receiving-fixed should also be tradeable at near-equivalent edge, modulo Boros's fees)
- vs H3: low (different asset universe — stablecoins vs BTC/ETH)

### Tradeable from our setup? — NO without infra investment

Blockers:
- No Arbitrum wallet / no smart-contract execution infrastructure
- No wBTC custody
- No Boros API client written
- Our 3 paper bots (H31/H34/H3) target ALT universe; Boros only has BTC/ETH (and 4 others mentioned: BNB, SOL, DOGE, HYPE — but limited liquidity)

### Tradeable as INDICATOR even without trading Boros directly?

YES — this is the cheaper read. Boros YU implied APR is a **live market-implied forecast of future funding rate**. We can READ this number from Boros (via Arbitrum RPC, no execution needed) and use it:

1. **Signal for H34**: if Boros implied APR for venue X > Boros implied APR for venue Y by >5%, that's the market's consensus that funding rate gap will persist → potentially leading indicator for our H34 entries
2. **Signal for H38**: large implied APR jump on a single venue may precede magnitude-trigger events
3. **Calibration cross-check**: validates whether our own H37 funding predictor formula agrees with market pricing

→ Cheap read-only integration would let us test these signals without execution infra. Filed as H_BOROS_INDICATOR sub-hypothesis.
