## Part 1 — Data inputs and method

### Inputs

```

  cols used: ex, sym, entry_ts, direction (1=LONG below$1 / -1=SHORT above$1), pnl_pct_net,

             cat_50bp, c2_max_div, c2_100bp, abs_rate
```

### Method (4 parts)

**Part A — Day-level co-occurrence.** Restrict both datasets to overlap window 2025-11-03 → 2026-05-01 (180 days). Group events by `dt.floor('D')`. Build 4-cell co-occurrence cross-tab. Compute observed/expected ratio under independence.

**Part B — Conditional means + correlation.** Compute daily aggregates per edge (n, mean PnL, sum PnL, mean/max |rate| for H38). Compute Pearson correlations on (i) overlap-day pairs only, (ii) full grid with zero-fill on non-event days, (iii) weekly aggregation. Conditional means: H38 events on H3-days vs non-H3-days; H3 events on H38-days vs non-H38-days (trivial since H38 fires every day in window).

**Part C — Lagged correlation.** For k ∈ {−3, −2, −1, 0, +1, +2, +3} days, compute corr(H3-day indicator at d, H38 metric at d+k). Tests whether H3 precedes/follows H38 spikes.

**Part D — Tail-risk overlap.** Identify top-20 H38 days by max-|rate|. Check how many are H3-days vs expected under independence. Also check H38 chronic-discount cluster (top10 syms RIVER/PIPPIN/ENSO/ORCA/RAVE/DRIFT/ENJ/LYN/BEAT/ZKP) aggregate mean PnL on-H3 vs off-H3.

**Sanity:** H3 direction-split (LONG=below-$1, SHORT=above-$1) — does cluster effect differ by depeg direction?

**Key observation:** H38 fires on 100% of days in the overlap window. The original "same-day intersection ratio" test is degenerate — every H3-day is trivially an H38-day. **Implication for methodology:** day-level overlap tests are uninformative when one edge is dense and the other is sparse. The right tests are PnL-conditional, lagged correlation, and tail-risk overlap (Parts B/C/D below).

**Operational interpretation:**

- **H31 ↔ H38** (+0.54): same edge family, share single risk-budget bucket (cycle 1100 verdict).
- **H31 ↔ H3** (−0.31): counter-cyclical pair, portfolio variance reduction when both deployed.
- **H38 ↔ H3** (+0.04): orthogonal, independent risk bucket (this cycle).
- Therefore: **H3 is the natural diversifier for both H31 (counter-cyclical) and H38 (orthogonal)**. This is the cleanest portfolio-level case yet for H3's strategic value.

### Sample-size constraints

- H3 24mo dataset has 129 vol-vetted G_paper events; only **14** fall in H38 overlap window. Statistical power to detect anything stronger than |Δ| ≥ 0.5pp at p<0.10 is low.
- H38 6mo dataset (Nov 2025 – May 2026) cannot be retroactively extended without re-fetching cross-ex funding history (cycle 1750 work, ~2h re-run if desired).
- Useful follow-ups blocked by data: (a) lagged H38→H3 future direction (H3 is post-event mean-rev, hard to assess "predicted" direction); (b) H_OBS_USDE_OVERPEG_DAMPEN retest at n=20+ overpeg days.

### Why this finding is NOT a 4th edge

The question "is H3+H38 simultaneity a new operationally-tradeable signal" — answer **NO**:
- No PnL amplification on co-event days (gap −0.06pp = noise).
- No timing structure (lagged corr <0.15 all lags).
- No symbol-level systematic interaction (gaps non-monotone, cancel).
- The H_OBS_USDE_OVERPEG_DAMPEN sub-finding is an observation about regime, not a trade entry rule (it would suggest *avoiding* H38 entries on USDe-overpeg days, n=4 too thin to act on).

### Methodology Lesson #25 CANDIDATE

**When testing co-occurrence between a RARE-event edge (≤10 days/mo) and a CONTINUOUS-event edge (≥30 days/mo), day-level intersection is structurally uninformative. The dense edge subsumes the sparse one by construction.** Required reframing:
1. Conditional-mean of dense-edge metrics on sparse-edge-days (Welch-t test).
2. Lagged correlation across ±3-5 day window.
3. Tail-risk overlap (top-K dense events' coincidence rate vs expected under independence).
4. Per-symbol or per-sub-cluster decomposition (regime/cluster-conditional discrimination).

Generalizes Meth #21 (correlation framing depends on post-filter LHS) to: **correlation framing also depends on event sparsity**. Sparse-edge daily corr expresses signal; dense-edge daily corr washes out by event density. Apply this reframe to any cross-edge test where one side fires <weekly and the other side fires daily.

Promotion path: 1-2 more cycles applying the 4-part reframe to another sparse×dense pair (e.g., H3 × H34, H31 × any minute-resolution dense indicator).
