## M1 — Partial hedge ratio sweep

### Setup

Data: `/tmp/h31_net.parquet` (154 events, of which 116 are LONG-only `pre_rate<0`). Each row has:
- `fund_cum4periods`: cumulative funding earned by LONG-perp over 4 funding periods after entry
- `price_4h`: perp price change (positive = perp rose, gain for LONG)
- `borrow_4h_pct`: borrow cost on spot leg over 4h hold
- `net_4h_basis`: pre-computed 100%-hedged net (= fund − borrow − 10bp slip)
- `net_4h_unhedged`: pre-computed 0%-hedged net (= fund + price)

Reconstruction (recon_basis = fund − borrow − 10bp): matches `net_4h_basis` exactly to 1e-3 precision. ✅

### Trade definition for general h ∈ [0, 1]

```
pnl_h(event, h) = fund_cum4periods                       # funding always captured 100%
                + (1-h) * price_4h                        # price exposure scaled by (1-h)
                - h * (borrow_4h_pct + SPOT_SLIP_PCT)    # hedge cost on h fraction (10bp)
```

Holding period: 4 funding periods (typically 16h on 4h cadence after shortening).

### Sweep results (full sample, n=116)

```
h_pct  mean_pct  std_pct  sharpe   WR    min_pct  max_pct
  0      4.10    25.69    0.16   49.1%   -43.8     137.9
 25      3.98    19.31    0.21   55.2%   -32.1     103.9
 50      3.86    12.95    0.30   60.3%   -20.5      70.0
 75      3.74     6.69    0.56   80.2%    -8.8      36.1
100      3.62     1.91    1.90  100.0%    +0.7       9.9
```

Sharpe is **monotone-increasing in h**, from 0.16 at no-hedge to 1.90 at full hedge. Mean PnL declines by only 49bp (4.10% → 3.62%) — that's exactly the borrow + slip cost paid on the hedge leg. The std collapse from 25.7% → 1.9% (13× reduction) absolutely dominates.

Fine grid (5pp steps) confirms monotonicity — there is NO intermediate sweet spot. Even h=95% (5pp under-hedge) gives Sharpe 1.58 with min −0.5% (small risk reintroduced), worse than h=100%'s structural Sharpe 1.90 with min +0.7%.

### Walk-forward TRAIN(70%) / TEST(30%) confirms structural h=100% winner

```
              TRAIN n=81             TEST n=35
h=  0%  mean +1.28% sharpe 0.10   mean +10.63% sharpe 0.26
h= 50%  mean +2.49% sharpe 0.36   mean  +7.04% sharpe 0.34
h=100%  mean +3.69% sharpe 1.87   mean  +3.45% sharpe 1.96
```

Importantly: the unhedged TEST mean (+10.63%) is a TEST-pocket artifact (TRAIN +1.28%, ratio 8.3×, same flavor as R14). The hedged variant shows TRAIN +3.69 / TEST +3.45 gap 0.24pp — exceptionally stable.

### Mechanism — why no "directional gain" exists

User hypothesis decomposed: "negative funding pre-event → shorts cover post-event → BUY perp → perp rallies → unhedged LONG captures both funding AND price." Empirical breakdown:

```
UP subset   (price_4h > 0):  n=42   mean price move +19.22%
DOWN subset (price_4h ≤ 0):  n=74   mean price move -10.36%
```

74 of 116 events (64%) see perp FALL post-interval-shortening. Distribution is right-skewed but not strongly positive on average (full-sample mean price_4h = +0.35%, dominated by a few large rally events with substantial offset from many smaller declines). 

At hedge ratios 0-75%, the few outsized rally events (top: +137.9%) drive a positive but very high-variance mean. Under any practical capital allocation (Kelly fraction or per-trade size cap), the resulting strategy is unhedgeable from a risk-management standpoint — at 0% hedge you lose 6.85% per DOWN event mean and have 51% loss rate on the trade.

**The "BUY perp on short-cover" pressure exists for some events but doesn't aggregate to a tradeable directional bias on the H31 universe.** The 100% hedged variant collects funding cleanly across all regimes (UP, DOWN, sideways) without taking the directional bet.

### M1 verdict

**REJECT** the partial-hedge variant. H31 paper-stream spec stays at LONG-perp + 100%-SHORT-spot basis-hedge. The Sharpe 1.84 / +3.45% mean / 100% WR / structural-WR mechanism is the optimum, not a starting point to be improved by selective unhedging.

## M3 — Methodology #14 retroactive on H31

### Categorization approaches

Two independent operationalizations:

**Approach A — other-shortening coincidence**. For each event `(ts, primary_ex, sym_norm)`, check whether ANOTHER exchange also shortened the funding interval on the same `sym_norm` within ±24h. Uses `/tmp/h31_results.parquet` (213 shortenings, 5 exchanges). CONFIRMED if `other_shortenings_24h ≥ 1`, else SOLO.

**Approach B — other-funding-stress at event time**. For each event, look at the funding period closest to `ts` (±6h) on the OTHER exchanges, take the same-direction (negative-rate) value, and flag if any other exchange shows `|rate| ≥ 30bp` (B30) or `≥ 50bp` (B50) at that moment. CONFIRMED if any other exchange is simultaneously stressed, else SOLO.

A is the literal Meth #14 analog (cross-venue coincidence of the same EVENT type). B is broader — captures "other venues also have crowded one-side OI" even if they haven't yet shortened.

### Category counts (n=116)

```

Approach B30 (other ≥30bp ±6h):       CONFIRMED  96   SOLO  20
Approach B50 (other ≥50bp ±6h):       CONFIRMED  93   SOLO  23
```

H31 universe is heavily CONFIRMED by either definition — funding-stress in this universe is overwhelmingly multi-venue. That itself is a finding: interval-shortening events are NOT a SOLO-rich subset like H3 depegs were (where ~28% of vetted events were SOLO).

### Mechanism — why Meth #14 inverts here

For H3 depeg arb (cycle 2300):
- Trade STRUCTURE: betting on price reversion (LONG depegged spot → wait for revert to $1).
- SOLO event: idiosyncratic orderbook noise → fast revert (median 2h).
- CONFIRMED event: systemic stress on multiple rails → slow revert (median 112h), may hit max-hold loss.
- → SOLO outperforms.

For H31 basis-hedged interval-shortening (this cycle):
- Trade STRUCTURE: capturing funding payments while delta-neutral to price.
- SOLO event: only this venue's funding is stressed → magnitude moderate → smaller funding payment.
- CONFIRMED event: multiple venues have crowded shorts → bigger funding magnitudes (mechanism: when crowding is real, it pushes funding to its cap on each venue simultaneously) → bigger funding payment.
- Price-drift direction is HEDGED OUT → CONFIRMED's systemic price drift is irrelevant.
- → CONFIRMED outperforms.

**Cross-validating signal: |price_4h| dispersion confirms the mechanism.**
```
SOLO:       |price_4h| mean=6.10%  median=4.94%  p90=12.96%
CONFIRMED:  |price_4h| mean=18.13% median=11.81% p90=37.95%
```

CONFIRMED events have 3× larger price-drift dispersion — i.e., the price moves a lot more during the hold. For an unhedged strategy this would be deadly (huge variance, bad direction often). For the hedged strategy, this price dispersion is entirely absorbed by the spot leg, leaving only the funding payment, which is bigger when crowding is bigger.

### Methodology Lesson #17 (CANDIDATE) — Meth #14 sign depends on trade structure

> **Cross-venue coincidence is a CONFIRMED-positive signal for HEDGED funding-capture
> strategies (bigger crowd → bigger funding → bigger captured PnL with same WR).
> It is a CONFIRMED-negative signal for UNHEDGED mean-reversion strategies (bigger
> crowd → slower revert → price-drift loss accumulates over hold).**
>
> Sign of Meth #14 = sign(d PnL / d coincidence) determined by:
> - HEDGED trade: + (more is better)
> - UNHEDGED mean-rev trade: − (more is worse)
> - UNHEDGED trend-following trade (untested): expected +
>
> When applying Meth #14 to a new instrument or trade variant, classify the trade
> structure first; do NOT assume the H3 sign convention.

This is the second refinement of Meth #14 (first was Meth #16 candidate: stable-class boundary for centralized vs distributed redemption). The original cycle 2300 Meth #14 framing implicitly assumed the UNHEDGED mean-rev case — its applicability to other trade structures requires the M3-style empirical re-derivation.

### Should we ship H31_CONFIRMED_ONLY as a tighter variant?

CONFIRMED-only is Sharpe 2.07 vs full-universe Sharpe 1.84 (delta +0.23, +12%). Throughput cost: 116 → 72 events, −38% drop. Mean PnL up: +3.52% → +4.03% (+50bp, +14%).

Trade-off math: at full universe, 116 events × $X notional × +3.52% = $4.08X aggregate annual.
At CONFIRMED-only, 72 events × $X notional × +4.03% = $2.90X aggregate annual.

**Capital efficiency: 116 events × 3.52% = 408bp-events vs 72 × 4.03% = 290bp-events.** Full universe gives more aggregate edge despite lower per-event mean.

If notional is the binding constraint (e.g. per-event capital limit on liquidity), tighter variant doesn't help; full universe wins. If aggregate capital deployment is the constraint, the tighter variant doesn't help either. The CONFIRMED-only variant would only win if there's a meaningful execution failure rate proportional to event count (e.g. exchange downtime, slip slippage on micro-caps) that disproportionately affects SOLO events.

**Decision**: KEEP full-universe H31 paper-stream as-is. Optionally tag CONFIRMED in trade metadata for future per-category live-monitoring. Do NOT add a CONFIRMED-only variant to the bundle.

## What we LEARNED today

1. **The H31 paper-stream spec is structurally optimal among the user-proposed alternatives.** Partial hedge cannot beat full hedge on Sharpe in this dataset; the unhedged variance penalty dominates any directional capture. The user's micro-structure critique was important to test, but the data closes it: there's no captured directional gain hiding in basis movements.

2. **Methodology #14 has a SECOND boundary (after Meth #16 stable-class boundary)**: trade-structure dependence. SOLO outperforms only for unhedged mean-reversion strategies. For hedged funding-capture, CONFIRMED outperforms. Filed as Meth #17 candidate. This refines our intuition for which legacy rejecteds (R1-R18) are worth re-testing under the SOLO/CONFIRMED lens.

3. **H31 universe is overwhelmingly CONFIRMED** (62% by Approach A, 82% by Approach B30). Interval-shortening events are inherently multi-venue phenomena — when a coin develops one-sided OI imbalance enough to drive funding to interval-change-trigger levels, that imbalance typically shows on multiple venues simultaneously. SOLO interval-shortenings exist (n=44/116) but are not the bulk.

4. **Price-move asymmetry in H31 universe is mild and NOT in the user-hypothesized direction.** 64% of LONG-only events see perp FALL (not rise) over the 4-period hold. The "short-squeeze → BUY perp" mechanism happens in ~36% of events but is more than offset by the bear cases.

5. **The fact that hedged WR is structurally 100% across SOLO AND CONFIRMED is genuine and re-confirmed.** Min funding magnitude (80bp) > worst-case basis cost (~70bp = 30bp/day borrow over 4h hold + 30bp slip) for both subsets. This is the algebraic-WR property cycle 2300 documented — and it survives all M1/M3 stratifications.

6. **The R2 SOLO retest directive (HYPOTHESIS_R2_SOLO_RETEST.md) is now better-informed**: knowing that Meth #14's sign flips by trade structure, R2 is an UNHEDGED scalp → if Meth #14 applies it should be in the H3 direction (SOLO outperforms). But we should also explore the HEDGED R2 variant (if such a thing makes sense for fair-price scalping — unclear what would be hedged against). Defer formal R2 test to next cycle as originally planned.

7. **Two more directions ruled out for free.** M1 and M3 both came back negative. We don't have new edges to add but the alternatives directory is shorter — every refutation hardens the existing portfolio.
