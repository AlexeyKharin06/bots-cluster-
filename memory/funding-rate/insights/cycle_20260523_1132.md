A median-vol-of-zero means **half the 5m bars print with no trades** — the API emits a candle carrying the previous close, but no execution happened on that bar.

Drilling into the kucoin USDS +4.44% top event (2026-04-10):

```
ts            close     vol
1775826300000 0.9959    0.00
... 11 consecutive dead bars at 0.9959 ...
1775829600000 1.0000    830.43  ← 50bp re-peg bar with thin vol
1775829900000 1.0479    3.44    ← entry @ 480bp DEPEG on 3.4 units of vol
1775830200000 1.0010    4.29    ← "re-peg" reached in 5min
1775830500000 1.0470    15.57   ← back to depeg
```

This is a **print-only spike**: 3.4 units of trading volume (~$3-4 worth) moved the quoted price 4.8% from peg. The strategy's "+4.4% gain" is mathematically real but operationally impossible — there is no executable depth at those prices, and the price reverts on similarly tiny volume.

Similar pattern on kucoin USDS -3.39%/-3.80% events. **All 4 kucoin USDS events fall on ghost prints.**

## Multi-venue coincidence check (H3-FOLLOWUP-3 partial)

PYUSD-only: 24 events across kucoin + bybit, on 22 unique days. Multi-venue same-day: only **2 days** had both venues triggering. The remaining 20 days are single-venue events.

This is informative for H3-FOLLOWUP-3 (multi-venue coincidence filter to weed out single-venue glitches): on PYUSD, requiring multi-venue agreement would discard 90% of events. That's the wrong tuning for PYUSD — real PYUSD depegs are venue-specific because the two venues have different liquidity profiles and arb pathways. Conclusion: **the multi-venue filter should not be applied blindly across all stables**; it's better-motivated for stables whose primary arb is on-chain (USDe redemption, USDD TRON-bridge) where a real depeg should be visible on multiple CEX listings, vs. for stables whose primary arb is venue-specific (PYUSD/Paxos issuer-redemption flow).

## Why Gate API blocked

Gate's `/api/v4/spot/candlesticks` rejected requests with "Candlestick too long ago. Maximum 10000 points ago." 10,000 × 5min = 35 days max lookback. This wiped our intended GHO/CRVUSD/SUSD/CUSD/USDG-on-Gate fetch. Workaround for future: stitch 5min bars from last 35d only, then fall back to 1h for older bars (Gate may allow longer history at lower resolution).

## What this means for H3 in HARDEN-AND-DEPLOY

1. **PYUSD is a viable add** to the H3 universe, on both KuCoin and Bybit:
   - +24 events / 12mo at 50bp trigger (kucoin 20 + bybit 4)
   - Mean +0.49% (about half the parent H3 mean — regulated-stable arb resolves faster)
   - 100% WR survives, structurally consistent with H3 mechanism
   - Adds first non-(USDe/USDD/USDC/USDP/TUSD/FDUSD) exposure → diversifies issuer-tail risk

2. **The cycle's strongest finding is the operational caveat, not the throughput add.** Most "synthetic stable" pairs on CEX are extremely thin. A naive H3 paper-stream that triggers on all stables would burn execution on ghost prints. **Volume gate is mandatory**: e.g., require entry-bar notional ≥ $500 AND median 5m vol > 0 in past 7 days for the (ex, sym) combo. The parent H3 universe (USDC/USDP/TUSD/FDUSD/USDD/USDe) is large-cap-stable + active-trading; the long-tail synthetic stables aren't a free expansion.

3. **GHO, USDM, USDS-kucoin, CRVUSD, SUSD, CUSD, RLUSD, USDtb cannot be assessed** with available CEX 5m OHLCV. GHO trades primarily on Curve+Uniswap on-chain; CRVUSD too. These would need a DEX TWAP from GeckoTerminal / TheGraph / Helius and on-chain swap-cost modeling. Significant work; deferred.

4. **bybit XUSD looks tantalizing** (30 events, +0.81% mean) but at 82.7% zero-vol bars and most events on sub-$100-volume wicks, it's a ghost-print universe. Excluded from paper-stream consideration.

## Methodology lesson #13

**Spot-OHLCV-based backtests of long-tail crypto stablecoins emit phantom events.** API endpoints carry forward the previous close on bars with no trades, but the strategy's downstream code can't tell. A wick from `0.996 → 1.048 → 0.997` on 3 units of volume looks identical in OHLCV to a real 480bp depeg-and-recovery — but the latter is tradable at quoted prices and the former is not.

**Rule going forward**: any new stablecoin (or thin asset class) backtest must include a volume gate at the event-detection layer. Suggested floor: entry-bar notional > $500 in same-venue currency. Without this, we will systematically overstate throughput on illiquid universes.

This applies retroactively: would re-check whether existing H3 USDD/USDe events (especially the +9.96% headline event in the 500bp+ bucket) trade on real volume. Filed as **H3-FOLLOWUP-6: vol-vetted re-run of 24mo H3** for next cycle.

## Verdict

**A3 = PARTIAL POSITIVE.** H3 mechanism generalizes to PYUSD cleanly (24 events at +0.49%/event, 100% WR). Modest throughput add to H3 universe (~1.7 events/month in the 50bp tier), but **directional confirmation** that H3 isn't USDe/USDD-specific — the same mint-arb/redemption mechanism resolves depegs across regulated synthetic stables of comparable structure.

**The cycle's bigger contribution is Methodology #13 (vol-gate rule).** Without it, ~9 of 63 events (14%) were ghost prints on dead tape — and that's only after we filtered to known-active venues. On true long-tail stables (GHO/USDM/CRVUSD), the proportion would be higher. The H3 paper-stream spec is updated to require entry-bar volume verification at the trigger layer.

Edge 3 remains validated; this cycle hardens its operational definition rather than changing its strategic status.
