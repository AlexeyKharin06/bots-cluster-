# Cycle 20260524_0500 — H3-FU-4 stable-perp basis variant: PERP UNIVERSE = ONLY BYBIT USDe; reframed as TAIL-RISK INSURANCE, not yield boost

## TL;DR

Exhaustive discovery across 7 major CEXes (Binance/Bybit/OKX/Gate/Bitget/MEXC/Hyperliquid) found **exactly one** stablecoin-perpetual listing on any venue: **Bybit USDEUSDT-PERP**. USDD, USDP, TUSD, FDUSD, PYUSD have ZERO perp listings anywhere. So H3-FU-4 perp-basis variant collapses to USDe-only scope, applicable to 22 of 129 vetted H3 events (17%).

Tested USDe-PERP behavior during all 21 covered KuCoin spot-depeg events (lost 1 to pre-listing 2024-08-07): **Bybit perp stays within ±10bp of $1 even during the dramatic +1246bp / +852bp spot spikes on KuCoin**. The depegs are venue-isolated; perp doesn't track. Therefore:

- **Substitute variant** (replace SHORT-spot leg with SHORT-perp for direction=-1 events): mean **-0.004% / WR 27%** — catastrophic, REJECTED. Perp doesn't move when spot does, so substitution captures nothing.
- **Funding capture variant**: Bybit USDe-PERP funding is too tight (mean -0.47bp/8h, max |21bp|, NO events ≥30bp out of 1713 fundings) to provide meaningful carry. REJECTED.
- **Delta-hedge variant** (LONG-spot + SHORT-perp, or vice versa): combined PnL **+1.997% / WR 95.2%** vs spot-only +2.00% / WR 100%. Gross is essentially equal — provides TAIL-RISK INSURANCE against Ethena protocol collapse at near-zero baseline cost (~10bp/leg extra slip = 20bp round-trip), at small WR cost (95.2% vs 100%, one event becomes flat).

**Verdict**: H3-FU-4 ACCEPTED as **optional tail-risk insurance overlay** on H3_DEPEG_PAPER USDe subset only (22/129 = 17% of universe). NOT a yield-boost. REJECTED as substitute leg, REJECTED as funding-capture vehicle.

**New methodology lesson #15**: A perp leg on a venue-isolated depeg-arb provides only tail-risk insurance (delta-neutrality against systemic protocol failure), not arb. Confirms Methodology #14 from a third independent vantage point — Bybit-perp non-movement during KuCoin spot depegs is direct evidence of venue-isolation.

## Variant C: DELTA-HEDGE (LONG-spot + SHORT-perp, or SHORT-spot + LONG-perp)

```
n=21 combined
combined_basis (= spot_pnl - perp_pnl with directional sign):
  mean +1.997% / median +1.020% / WR 95.2% / max +11.053% / min -0.020%

By direction:
  LONG (dir=+1, spot<$1) n=10:
    spot-only net orig: +0.891% / WR 100%
    combined_basis:     +0.835% / WR  90%  (1 event ties to 0 — perp tracked spot fully)
  SHORT (dir=-1, spot>$1) n=11:
    spot-only net orig: +3.008% / WR 100%
    combined_basis:     +3.054% / WR 100%

Cost adjustment: +20bp round-trip slip for 2 extra perp legs
  → Net combined ≈ +1.80% mean vs spot-only +2.00% → marginal ~10% PnL haircut
```

Combined_basis numerically ≈ spot-only because perp barely moves. But the STRUCTURAL value is delta-neutrality against systemic moves:

- If USDe permanently re-rates (e.g. Ethena protocol failure causes USDe → $0.50): spot-only LONG position takes the full -50% loss. Spot+perp hedge: spot loses, perp gains (perp also re-rates if underlying is the same asset) → near-zero net loss.
- Routine KuCoin venue-local depegs: combined captures the same +2% as spot-only minus ~20bp extra slip.

This is **tail-risk insurance with low baseline cost** — the perp leg pays for itself only in protocol-failure scenarios.

## Methodology lesson #15 — perp leg on venue-isolated depeg = insurance, not arb

**For depeg arb where the depegging instrument is the SAME ASSET on a different venue with a PERP listing:**
1. Perp tracks fair-value of the asset, not the local depegged spot. So perp can't substitute the local-spot trade leg.
2. Perp funding reflects perp-spot basis on the perp's own venue, not the depeg arb opportunity on the other venue. So perp funding can't be harvested for the depeg.
3. The delta-hedge variant (LONG/SHORT spot + opposite-direction perp) provides systemic-risk neutrality at the cost of an extra 10-20bp/leg slip. Useful only if the underlying issuer-tail risk is material.

Filed as Methodology #15: "Perp legs on venue-isolated depeg arbs are tail-risk insurance, not yield boosters."

## What we LEARNED today

1. **The stable-perpetual universe is essentially {Bybit USDeUSDT-PERP}**. Across 7 major CEXes, no other stablecoin has perp listings. This means any future "use perp leg" strategy for stable arb is constrained to USDe on Bybit only.

2. **USDe-PERP on Bybit is a fair-value tracker, not a local-spot-depeg tracker**. When KuCoin USDe spot spiked to 1.12 (+1246bp), Bybit perp was at 0.9999. This is a sharp demonstration that perp arb closes any meaningful perp-spot gap on the perp's venue within minutes — and that the KuCoin depeg has zero gravitational pull on a Bybit perp because the perp isn't bridged to KuCoin spot liquidity.

3. **The 2-3× yield boost hypothesis from cycle 2300 is REJECTED**. The "capture perp funding leg on top of spot reversion" thesis assumed perp funding would be sizable during depegs (40-100bp would be needed for a meaningful boost). Reality: perp funding never exceeded 21bp/8h, capped roughly at the perp-spot basis on Bybit itself.

4. **The perp leg has narrow real value: TAIL-RISK INSURANCE for Ethena protocol risk**. At ~20bp extra round-trip cost (small relative to the +2% per-event base PnL), the delta-hedge transforms H3-USDe from "USDe-spot directional" to "USDe cross-venue spread", removing protocol-tail-risk component.

5. **Third independent line of evidence for Methodology #14**: KuCoin USDe depegs are venue-isolated SOLO events. Bybit-perp non-movement is independent confirmation of the cross-venue-spot finding from cycle 2300.
