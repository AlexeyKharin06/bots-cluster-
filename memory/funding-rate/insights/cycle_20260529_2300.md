## Backtest (A) — CONDITIONAL: C2-early entry on H31 events

### Method
For each of 116 H31 LONG events:
1. event_ts, primary_ex, sym = event row from h31_net (direction==1).
2. Search c2_wide for the earliest hour-bucket in `[event_ts − 12h,
   event_ts − 1h]` (strict-lead) where `div ≥ threshold` AND primary_ex has
   funding data for that hour.
3. If C2 trigger found: compute extra_funding =  −Σ(rates) on primary_ex's
   funding postings in `(C2_ts, event_ts]` from multi_ex_funding_180.
4. Estimated C2-early net = H31_baseline_net + extra_funding × CAPTURE_RATIO.
5. CAPTURE_RATIO (empirical at H31 baseline event point) = 0.937 (= mean
   `net_4h_basis` / mean `fund_cum4periods` = 3.516% / 3.751%). Stress sweep:
   0.94, 0.70, 0.50, 0.30, 0.00.

### Results (face-value, cr=0.937)
| threshold | n_trig | coverage | lead_h | extra_fund | extra_net | lift universe | lift triggered |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| p90 (4.3 bp) | 86 | 74.1% | 9.5 h | +2.74% | +2.57% | **+1.91 pp** | +2.57 pp |
| p95 (7.7 bp) | 79 | 68.1% | 8.7 h | +2.74% | +2.57% | **+1.75 pp** | +2.57 pp |
| p99 (28 bp)  | 56 | 48.3% | 6.5 h | +2.37% | +2.22% | **+1.07 pp** | +2.22 pp |

WR = 100% maintained at all thresholds (every H31 event already has
net_4h_basis > 0, and extra_funding is positive whenever C2 fires).

### Walk-forward (50/50 temporal split, median ts 2026-03-04 20:00)
| thr | TRAIN h31 / c2_early / lift | TEST h31 / c2_early / lift |
|---|---|---|
| p90 | 3.44% / 5.34% / +1.91 | 3.60% / 5.50% / +1.91 |

All three thresholds — both halves positive lift, p95 TEST > TRAIN (Meth #12

| 0.00 | 0      | 0      | 0      |

Every threshold passes the +30bp gate even at cr=0.30 (most pessimistic

## Backtest (B) — UNCONDITIONAL: C2 as standalone (live-deployable)

### Why this matters
Conditional (A) implicitly time-travels — it assumes we know which C2 triggers
will be followed by an H31 shortening. Operationally we don't. Live deployment
requires: scan c2_wide, trigger at div >= threshold, hold until either a
shortening fires OR max-hold timeout. Most triggers (~96% at p95, ~88% at p99
within 24h) DO NOT see a shortening — those convert the trade into a pure
chronic-discount funding-harvest, which may or may not net out positive.

### Method
- Scan all (sym, hour) where `div ≥ threshold` AND most-negative ex non-NaN.
- Dedup per (sym, primary_ex) with 24h cooldown.
- Enter LONG perp on most-negative ex. Hold for `min(24h, shortening_ts + 4h)`.
- Funding collected = `-Σ(rates)` on primary_ex over the hold window.
- Net = funding_collected × cr − 40bp round-trip cost (5bp entry slip + 5bp
  exit slip + 5bp taker fee, both legs = 40bp total).
- Capture-ratio sweep + 50/50 walk-forward + permutation null
  (shuffle div within sym, take 1000 random trigger cells).

### Results (after dedup)
| threshold | n_trig | precision_shorten (24h) | mean_fund | mean_hold |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| p95 | **5,047** | 3.43% | +1.15% | 23.6 h |
| p99 | **1,063** | 12.0% | +3.04% | 22.2 h |

Precision-shorten roughly matches cycle 1700 lift numbers (p95 24h lift ~28×
baseline 0.122%, p99 24h ~12.0%/0.122% = ~98× → checks out within base-rate
window-vs-window noise).

### SHORTEN vs NO_SHORTEN split (the critical decomposition)

| p95 | 173 | +4.90% | 4,874 | +1.01% |

SHORTEN-subset numbers REPRODUCE the H31 baseline magnitude (+4.9%/4.5% gross

### Permutation null (decisive integrity check, per Meth #30/#31)
Shuffle `div` within each `sym_norm` (preserve marginal distribution); sample
1000 perm triggers; compute same fund_collected metric:

| threshold | real_mean_fund | perm_mean_fund | real / perm |
|---|---|---|---|
| p95 | 1.146% | 0.596% | 1.92× |
| p99 | 3.040% | 0.971% | 3.13× |

Permutation null is NOT zero — randomly selected "most-negative-ex" cells
still collect ~60-100bp of funding on average. This is the residual tautology
flagged by Meth #31 (selecting by funding sign mechanically creates positive
funding-harvest expectation). But the real signal IS materially above null:
p99 real/perm = 3.1×, p95 = 1.9×. The C2 signal genuinely concentrates
above-random funding magnitudes.

## Joint interpretation

### What this cycle establishes
1. **C2-early-entry CONDITIONAL on H31 events PASSES** the gate with margin
   across all thresholds and capture-ratio stress levels. Lift +34 to 191 bp
   per H31 event. This is an operational enhancement: enter at the earliest
   C2 trigger in the lead window instead of at the H31 event timestamp.
2. **C2 UNCONDITIONAL at p99 PASSES** the gate but the signal is dominated by
   the same chronic-discount cluster underlying H31/H38; Sharpe is materially
   worse; TRAIN > TEST.
3. The p99 unconditional decomposition shows that *most* of the C2 signal's
   PnL comes from the 12% SHORTEN-subset which is approximately H31 in disguise
   (mean fund 4.46% ≈ H31's 3.75%). The 88% NO_SHORTEN subset contributes
   +2.85% gross funding — still positive but tightly tied to chronic-discount
   coins where the regime can revert.

### What this cycle does NOT establish (honest gaps)
- **The capture-ratio in the LEAD window** is the dominant unknown. I used
  the empirical 0.937 measured at the H31 event point (peak basis divergence
  → normalized) as the face-value assumption, then stress-tested down to 0.30.
  The TRUE answer requires fresh ±24h klines around each C2 trigger to
  measure basis drift directly. Stretching h31_klines won't help (only −1h to
  +9h coverage). This is the single highest-leverage follow-up.
- **NO_SHORTEN PnL geometry**: I treat extra_funding as positive (chronic
  discount harvest) but don't model the case where divergence STAYS WIDE
  through the hold-window timeout (basis drift LOCKS IN against the hedge
  before any renormalization). Real basis-drift on these triggers could be
  materially worse than the conservative cr=0.30 floor.
- **p99 throughput**: 1063 dedup'd triggers over 180 days = ~5.9/day across
  80 syms. That's high. But Sharpe 0.38 at cr=0.30 means we'd need very thin
  positions to keep portfolio variance bounded — promoting requires sizing
  discipline that the current spec doesn't address.

## Methodology candidate #32 (filed, not promoted)
For event-driven strategies with LEADING-INDICATOR signals, the right two-step
gate is: (1) "early-entry MARGINAL PnL on the SUPER-EVENT-confirmed subset"
(measures whether the lead window has tradeable funding harvest), and (2)
"unconditional standalone PnL on ALL signal triggers" (measures whether the
strategy is live-deployable without time-travel). A YES on (1) without a YES
on (2) means the indicator is a TIMING IMPROVEMENT for an existing edge, not
a 4th edge. This frames the H31 family question cleanly: H31's interval-change
trigger event is the SUPER-EVENT; C2 is its LEADING INDICATOR; the question
is whether the leading-indicator timing premium > 30bp/event marginal lift,
and whether unconditional standalone deploys positively.

Corroboration count: 1/2. C2 today corroborates the framework; needs one
more leading-indicator candidate to promote #32 to CONFIRMED.
