- 96.77% of H31-event-days (60/62) have at least one H38_CONFIRMED event the same day.
- Only 35.93% of H38_CONFIRMED-event-days (60/167) have an H31 event.

H38_CONFIRMED days are a strict superset (107 exclusive days). The 107 H38C-only days are when high-mag funding existed without an interval shortening.

Same-(exchange, symbol, hourly_bucket) match:


H31 LONG-only is essentially a 79%-overlap subset of H38_CONFIRMED-50bp. The other 21% are H31 events whose hourly funding rate at trigger time didn't meet H38's |rate|≥50bp threshold (interval shortenings that happen at small pre-rates).

### Symbol-cluster overlap

- H31 LONG syms: 58 unique
- H38_CONFIRMED-50bp syms: 93 unique
- Intersection: 41 (70.7% of H31 universe is inside H38C universe)

### Interpretation

The mechanistic relation is **superset, not orthogonal**. H38_CONFIRMED captures every high-mag negative-funding event regardless of interval-shortening status; H31 captures the smaller subset where the exchange also signals stress by shortening the interval. Per-event, H31's edge is +3.5% vs H38_CONFIRMED's +2.2% — interval-shortening behavior IS a quality marker on top of magnitude (consistent with cycle 1750 catalogue: H31 events = "high-magnitude-via-interval-trigger proxy").

**Portfolio implication.** Cycle 2300's verdict ("H38_CONFIRMED is NOT a 4th independent edge") is correlation-corroborated. Use as throughput-multiplier on the same hedged-funding-capture edge; do not count toward the 3-edge diversification gate.

**Counter-consideration.** Daily-corr 0.39 is below the 0.5 portfolio-rules-of-thumb threshold. For risk-budget purposes, H31 and H38_CONFIRMED could be allocated separately (40-60% overlap correlation means a 2-stream paper deploy still gets some smoothing). But the diversification "math" of 3-edges-required portfolios refers to mechanism-diversity, not just correlation — and mechanism is same.

## Part 2 — T1 mining expansion (deferred 3 cycles)

### Goal

Original tg_trade_cases.jsonl had n=80, all biased toward messages with explicit $-PnL claims (T2 input bias). Per cycle 1435 T2-classifier finding, the most common practitioner mechanism (pure-B basis-spread arb without interval-shortening) was likely *under-represented* in the n=80 sample. T1 mining: re-scan the full `tg_messages_historical.jsonl` (3,894 messages from 65-channel hub) with broader trade-event regex (RU + EN, mechanism keywords + trade-action OR $-mention).

### Regex/filter design

```
Mechanism keywords (RU+EN):
  funding: funding|фанд|фандос
  spread:  спред|spread|разлёт
  interval: интервал|cap[ -]?hit|сократили интервал
  hedge:   хедж|hedge|перп|perp
  depeg:   depeg|депег|usde|usdd
  listing: листинг|listing
  hack:    взлом|hack|exploit

Trade-action triggers (must have at least one):
  Action: зашорт|залонг|купил|продал|открыл|закрыл|поза|позиц|TP:|SL:|стоп|тейк|profit
  $-claim: $\d+|\d+$|\dK|\dк|пнл|pnl|прибыл

Coin ticker extract:
  Regex: \b[A-Z][A-Z0-9]{2,9}\b
  FP blocklist: ~80 acronyms/fiats/exchanges (USDT, BTC, ETH, RU, KRW, BINANCE, FDV, DAO, ADL, ...)
  Stripping: tickers ending in USDT/USDC
```
