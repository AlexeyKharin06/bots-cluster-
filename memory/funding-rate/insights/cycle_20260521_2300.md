## Method

For each of the 213 H31 shortening events:
1. Fetched 1h klines from the matching exchange's public futures API, window `event_ts ± 9h`. 5 fetchers (binance fapi, bybit v5 linear, okx history-candles, gate v4 futures, bitget v2 mix).
2. Determined strategy direction = `-sign(pre_rate_last)`:
   - `pre>0` → SHORT perp (collect positive funding from longs)
   - `pre<0` → LONG perp (collect negative funding by being on long side)
   - `pre==0` → skip (1 event)
3. Computed:
   - **price_PnL** = `direction × (close[ts+3h] - open[ts]) / open[ts]` for 4h horizon (similarly 1h/8h)
   - **funding_PnL** = `-direction × post_rate` (because if you're SHORT and rate>0, longs pay you the rate)
4. NET unhedged = funding_cum4 + price_4h − **10bp** (5bp/leg round-trip on single perp leg)
5. NET basis-hedged = funding_cum4 − borrow_cost − **20bp** (5bp/leg × 2 legs — perp + spot)
   - Borrow cost from `borrow_histories.jsonl` where available (72/116 LONG-side symbols); fallback 30bp/day for missing
6. Walk-forward TRAIN/TEST temporal cutoff at **2026-04-01** (same as prev cycle)

## Direction asymmetry — only LONG-side works

| direction | n | fund_cum4 | price_4h | net_unhedged | net_basis (recomputed below) | WR (net_unhedged) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| SHORT (pre>0) | 38 | **-2.94%** | +3.48% | +0.44% | strongly negative | 50.0% |
| LONG (pre<0) | 116 | **+3.75%** | +0.35% | +4.00% | +3.52% | 48.3% |

When `pre>0` and the exchange shortens the interval, the funding rate IMMEDIATELY FLIPS to deep negative on average (post mean = -2.94%). The "trapped longs" narrative reverses fast — the shortening signals the regime CHANGE. SHORT was the wrong side.

When `pre<0` (the dominant 76% case — exchanges shorten when shorts are trapped), the negative funding INTENSIFIES; LONG side keeps collecting. This is the directional regime persistence the GROSS analysis was implicitly riding.

**The strategy must filter: enter only when pre_rate < 0.**

## Caveats that still apply before live deploy

1. **Execution latency is critical.** H31 detection requires catching the exchange's interval-change announcement within seconds of the new interval taking effect. Some exchanges (Bybit, OKX) announce ahead of time; some (Binance, Gate) only show the new interval post-hoc. H29 poller must run at ≤30s cadence per exchange.

2. **Spot leg availability NOT verified per symbol.** Need to check at runtime: for each (exchange, symbol), is there a margin-spot pool to SHORT? If not, fallback to perp-perp cross-exchange hedge OR skip the event.

3. **Slippage on illiquid symbols UNKNOWN.** Some of the 122 H31 symbols are micro-caps (SIREN, PIPPIN, RIVER, RAVE — all in the rejected-microcap-expansion list R4). On those, real round-trip slippage may be 30-50bp, not 10-20bp. Stress test: even at 50bp slippage per leg (200bp round-trip on basis), edge survives at +2.5%/event.

4. **Borrow availability NOT verified.** 72/116 symbols are in our borrow_histories.jsonl (which was from Bybit). For other 44 symbols, fallback assumed 30bp/day. Some symbols may have NO spot lending — must runtime-check.

5. **Single sample period.** 180 days, one set of market conditions. The Nov-Apr period spanned a mostly bull regime; ranges and funding-rate-clamp behavior may differ in different regimes.

6. **The 100% WR is structural, not robust to model error.** It depends on: (a) the funding rate continuing in the same direction, (b) the basis-hedge holding to within a few bp of perfect (no large perp-spot divergence shocks), (c) borrow being available at expected rate. Each of (a)(b)(c) can fail in stress events. Real-world WR likely 85-95% after black-swan-style execution failures.

## Recommended path forward

1. **Paper-stream proposal:** `H31_BASIS_PAPER` — paper bot that on each detected interval-shortening event with `pre_rate<0` opens LONG-perp + SHORT-spot (or LONG-perp-A + SHORT-perp-B if spot unavailable) at $1 notional, exits after 4 funding periods. Logs realized funding, realized basis drift, execution slippage, borrow paid. Goal: n=30 paper trades over ~6 weeks to verify execution assumptions.

2. **H29 poller is the unblocker.** With our existing `multi_ex_funding_180.parquet` we can detect interval changes RETROSPECTIVELY but not in real time. A 30s-cadence poller on Binance/Bybit/OKX funding endpoints is the prerequisite for paper-stream OR live. Estimated implementation: 1 day.

3. **Validate spot-leg coverage.** One-shot script: for each of 5 exchanges, query `/spot/borrow-info` (or equivalent) for all H31 symbols. Output coverage matrix.

4. **Investigate perp-perp cross-exchange hedge as alternative.** Since we have funding rate data on 6 exchanges, we can compute the actual cross-exchange funding spread per (symbol, ts). If exchange B's funding for the same symbol at the same time is small/zero, perp-perp hedge captures most of the spread without borrow cost.

5. **Cross-validate with `expansion_funding.parquet` (35k rows).** That file has explicit `interval_min` column — would let us double-check the interval-detection method.
