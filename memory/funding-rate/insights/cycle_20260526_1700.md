## 6. METHODOLOGY #26 CANDIDATE — Filter recommendations from rejected variants do not transfer to validated variants without re-derivation

### Statement
When a sub-strategy is rejected, the diagnostic features identified as "the cause of failure"
(e.g., "nano-caps tank the unhedged variant") are properties of *that rejected trade structure*,
not of the underlying market. Before importing those features as filters into a *different,
validated* deployment, re-derive whether the same features are still problematic in the new
structure. The mechanism that caused the failure may already be neutralized by the validated
structure's different trade construction.

### Evidence (this cycle)

- MEGA_GRID downgrade identified `fp<$0.01` as nano-cap risk source (max loss −47% PIPPIN,
  −40% LYN in unhedged single-leg).
- BRIEF recommended `nano-cap filter` for "any H31 deployment".
- Test in this cycle: basis-hedged H31 absorbs the 25% NOM price-fall completely; b_milli tier
  delivers +3.46% / WR 100% / Sharpe 1.67 — *positive every event*.
- The hedge IS the mechanism that neutralizes the failure. Filtering nano-caps in the hedged
  variant is treating a symptom that doesn't exist in that structure.

### Generalization

| Source of failure   | Applies to variant   | Does NOT auto-apply to                                      |
|---------------------|----------------------|-------------------------------------------------------------|
| Price-direction loss| Unhedged             | Delta-neutral / basis / perp-perp (hedge absorbs)           |
| Funding-cost loss   | Funding-pay side     | Funding-collect side (sign-flip)                            |
| Cross-ex divergence | Single-ex unhedged   | Cross-ex multi-leg (already captures divergence as PnL)     |
| Liquidity wick      | High-frequency       | Low-frequency / longer hold (vol averages out)              |

### Operational rule (proposed)
A "filter from rejected variant" should be tested in the validated variant before being
imported. Default expectation: ~50% of such filters won't transfer (they were diagnostic of the
*rejected* structure, not of *market features*). When they DO transfer, document the *mechanism*
through which they apply in the new structure.

### Relation to prior methodology
This generalizes:
- **Meth #17** (CONFIRMED vs SOLO sign flips by trade structure) — same idea: filter sign is
  conditional on trade structure
- **Meth #23v2** (trigger × hedge_type 4-cell grid) — same idea: feature effect is
  conditional on hedge type
- **Meth #25** (sparse × dense correlation reframe) — same idea: filter shape changes with
  trade definition

Files **Meth #26** as candidate. Promotion gate: corroborate on another rejected-variant feature
transfer (e.g., R14 unhedged H31 → does the "high pocket noise" feature transfer to basis? — it
doesn't, we already know).
