## EXIT-LOGIC ALPHA — `early_exit_ratio_99` discovered

The most surprising finding this cycle. Same-token A vs B pair analysis:

Across all 611 token-pairs where both SNIPER_A and SNIPER_B traded the same entry:
- B better: 17 cases (3%)
- A better: 14 cases (2%)
- Tied (both rugged): 580 cases (95%)
- avg(B - A): +8.7pp

The 5% of non-rug cases is where the divergence matters. Looking at the 16 cases where B>>A by ≥50pp:
- **avg lift = +344pp**
- **ALL 16 exits used `early_exit_ratio_99` logic in B**

### Two patterns of B's superiority:

1. **Rug-avoidance**: B exits with +10 to +30% (small profit) on tokens that A holds through `rug_no_data` (instant -100%). Examples: OLEAF, HFRGE, APULS, SGRID, BNEX, HDOCK, VSPH, NVHIVE — all have B-exit `early_exit_ratio_99` at +10 to +30%, A holds and gets `rug_no_data` -100%. Diff +110-130pp.

2. **Big-winner capture**: Same exit logic catches multi-thousand-% peaks. MC +1268%, WORLDCUP +971%, PORTUGAL +906%, COMPUTE +856%, CATCOIN +542%. A's `sl` or `ride_exit_X%` either stop-losses out before pump or rides too long. B's early_exit_ratio_99 catches the peak.

The exit reason name suggests this is "exit when price reaches 99% of some ratio peak" — likely a moving-peak-based take-profit that:
- Fires early on weak entries (small profit before rug)
- Fires late on strong pumps (after multi-x extension)

This is **likely THE most important alpha** in the system. The same entry processed through A's exit = -100% rug. Same entry through B's exit = +50 to +1268%.

### Implication for H17
Since H17 entries are by definition microcap/decentralized/fresh, and SNIPER_B exits capture both rug-avoidance AND big-winner upside on these entries, the H17+SNIPER_B-exit combo is the right test path. SNIPER_A exit on H17 entries lost or barely-made-money on 5/6 tokens.

## What I learned (NEW for memory)
1. **Regime CAN flip 6h timescale** — confirmed by 0-2h previous pocket → 0-12h hostile reversion. Single-snapshot gate measurement is unreliable; need rolling.
2. **`both` signal is interpretation-dependent** — pure `both>=5` is a rug signal in aggregate; but `both>=5` AND smart=0 AND known=1 (i.e., when overlap wallets are the ONLY wallet flag) is the winning signature. The wallet-tagging classifier matters: the same wallets get attributed to `both` instead of being double-counted. This is a CLASSIFIER quirk that may be the actual edge.
3. **Exit logic dominates entry filtering on big winners** — H17 entries with A-exit lose money; same entries with B-exit make 6-figure %. The serial_sniper has 20+ streams with different exit configs — the alpha may be more in matching streams to entry-archetype than in finding new entry filters.
4. **Stream-level dedupe is mandatory but loses signal** — first-occurrence dedup typically keeps SNIPER_A (alphabetically first stream usually) which has worst exit logic, so dedup avg underestimates archetype-level alpha. For attribution, need stream-stratified dedup OR compute "best-stream PnL per token" as alpha measure.
