## Methodology — SOLO vs CONFIRMED on H38

### Data join
- `c8_fwd.parquet`: each row = one (exchange, sym_norm, ts_ms) H38 trigger event. Has `net_basis_conservative` (basis-hedged PnL net of fees / borrow) and `fund_collect` (raw funding capture).
- `c2_wide.parquet`: pivoted (sym_norm, ts_bucket_ms) × {binance, bitget, bybit, gate, hyperliquid, okx} cells = funding rate at hourly bucket on each exchange. NaN where exchange didn't list that sym at that hour.
- Hourly bucketing: `c8.ts_bucket = (c8.ts // 3,600,000) * 3,600,000` matches c2's grid exactly. 100% join coverage (5744/5744 unique (sym, ts_bucket) pairs in c8 are present in c2).

### Classification rule

For event at (E, S, T) where T is bucket-aligned:
- Look up c2_wide row at (S, T)
- Count OTHER exchanges (excluding E) with rate ≤ threshold
- If 0 → SOLO
- If ≥1 → CONFIRMED

Thresholds tested: -30bp (matches H38 trigger), -50bp (stricter), -100bp (much stricter).

### Why hourly bucket (not narrower ±30min)

H38 events fire on 8h-funding-cycle anchors → all rate snapshots ARE the once-per-hour fixing. Tighter window would just return zeros. The hourly bucket IS the natural sampling cadence.

## Decision: Meth #17 PROMOTED to CONFIRMED methodology

### Formal statement (Meth #17, post-cycle 2300)

> **For cross-venue COINCIDENCE filtering on funding-rate based strategies:**
> 
> If the trade is **HEDGED FUNDING-CAPTURE** (e.g. perp-spot basis, perp-perp delta-neutral): cross-venue CONFIRMATION of the trigger predicts HIGHER per-event PnL and HIGHER Sharpe than SOLO. Apply CONFIRMED gate. The basis IS the return; bigger systemic stress = bigger basis = more PnL.
> 
> If the trade is **UNHEDGED MEAN-REVERSION** (e.g. H3 stablecoin depeg, fair-price scalp): cross-venue CONFIRMATION predicts SLOW REVERT (real systemic stress takes time to redeem out); SOLO predicts FAST REVERT (idiosyncratic orderbook imbalance). Apply SOLO gate. Long holds in CONFIRMED tier accrue more time-cost / drawdown risk than the static mean-reversion thesis budgets for.
> 
> Sign-flip rule (Meth #17): the direction of cross-venue confirmation as a quality filter depends on whether the trade structure converts the same systemic-stress signal into a profit driver (hedged) or a holding-time penalty (unhedged).

### Provenance trail

- Meth #14 (cycle 2300, H3 depeg n=129): SOLO > CONFIRMED on unhedged mean-rev. +1.024pp gap. NEW finding at the time, sign was a surprise.
- Meth #17 candidate (cycle 1418, H31 LONG-only n=116): CONFIRMED > SOLO on hedged H31 basis-hedge. +1.346pp gap. Filed as candidate awaiting larger-n corroboration.
- **Meth #17 CONFIRMED (this cycle, H38 n=10,686)**: CONFIRMED > SOLO on hedged H38 basis-hedge. +1.090pp gap at 50bp, +1.680pp at 100bp. Direction holds on all 6 exchanges and walk-fwd. Sample is 92× larger than the candidate-stage H31 test.
- Meth #16 candidate (cycle 1100, PYUSD): SOLO ≈ CONFIRMED on centralized-redemption stables (B2B desk arbitrage equalizes both tiers). Stable-class-boundary for Meth #14. Meth #17 cross-check on centralized-redemption stables is not possible because Boros/Pendle doesn't tokenize them.

Three independent samples (H3, H31, H38) on two distinct trade structures all consistent with the sign-flip rule. Meth #17 is now CONFIRMED methodology.

1. **H38 spec gains a new tier**: H38_CONFIRMED-50bp = n=5324 / mean +2.23% / Sharpe 1.28 / WR 99% / ~890 events/month / slip-robust to 40bp. Strictly dominates the full H38 tier on Sharpe; sacrifices throughput.

2. **H31 paper spec re-validated** (cycle 1418 decision to keep CONFIRMED-only tier stands at decisive n): not a noise artifact.

3. **All FUTURE hedged-strategy backtests** must include cross-venue confirmation as a (negative-edge or positive-edge or no-effect) covariate. Meth #17 sign rule lets us predict direction in advance.
