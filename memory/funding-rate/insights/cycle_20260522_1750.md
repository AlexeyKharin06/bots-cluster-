## C9 — Borrow-spike Edge 3 candidate (REJECTED)

### Hypothesis
Per validated_signals_funding memory: borrow-rate spike >50% = "x10 lift on event probability."
Never walk-forward tested. Borrow spike implies short-demand surge → potential directional perp edge.

### Method
- `borrow_histories.jsonl`: 45 Bybit coins, 58,516 hourly borrow-rate observations,
  time range 2025-11-07 → 2026-05-06.
- Spike detector: `rate / rolling-24h-median ≥ 2.0` AND `rate ≥ 50bp/hour absolute`.
- Dedup by (coin, 4h-bucket) → 59 spike events across 11 coins.
- Cross-match to `multi_ex_funding_180.parquet` Bybit subset → only 5 coins had perp funding overlap
  (ENSO, KAT, ORCA, RED, STO) → 22 testable events.
- Fetched 1m Bybit klines `±9h` around each spike (11,902 klines).
- Computed LONG and SHORT perp PnL over 4h and 8h hold with 10bp slippage.

### Results

**LONG-perp post-spike (initial hypothesis):**
- Mean 4h net: **-2.80%**, WR 31.8% — WRONG direction.
- Mean price PnL: -3.21% → spike COINCIDES with continued price decline, not reversal.
- Funding collected: +0.51% (modest, doesn't compensate price drop).

**SHORT-perp post-spike (inverted hypothesis):**
- Mean 4h net: +2.60%, WR 63.6%, Sharpe 0.331.
- TRAIN n=15 mean 4h: +3.93%, WR 67% (encouraging).
- TEST n=7 mean 4h: **-0.24%**, WR 57% → **FAILS walk-forward**.
- TEST n=7 mean 8h: -2.39% → diverges further on longer hold.

### Verdict
**REJECTED as Edge 3.** 
- n=22 is too small for honest TRAIN/TEST verdict.
- Direction is correct (borrow-spike → price drops → SHORT works) but test pocket doesn't replicate train.
- Severe survivorship: only 5/45 coins had simultaneous Bybit perp funding data.
- Catalog as R16.

### Methodology lesson #9
**Borrow-rate spikes do NOT predict short-squeeze reversals.** Intuitive read (spike → squeeze
imminent → bullish) is WRONG. Mechanism is: spike happens because shorts are AGGRESSIVELY
correctly piling in. Price continues to drop. Edge would be SHORT-with-the-flow, not contrarian.
Not Edge 3 because n is too small to validate properly with only 5 overlap coins.

ALL 7,914+ high-mag candidates, not just the 116 interval-change subset. Question:
**Is basis-hedge on the WIDER universe a separate edge, or just H31 with relaxed trigger?**

### Method

- Define high-magnitude event: `|rate| ≥ 30bp` → 11,123 events.
