## The analysis: why cut60's uplift is smaller than it looks

trades.jsonl logs entry/exit/hold/exit_reason/max_adverse_pct/max_favorable_pct but NOT the
intermediate 60s-mark price. So cut60's exact effect on each ≥60s trade is **unobservable from
the log** — which is precisely why a live A/B is needed. But the *structure* of the ≥60s bucket
already bounds it.

### Meth #28 hold-time split (n=70, sum $10.569, WR 82.9%)
| bucket | n | WR | sum PnL | mean |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| sub-60s | 47 | 97.9% | +$23.64 | +$0.503 |
| ≥60s | 23 | 52.2% | −$13.07 | −$0.568 |

### exit_reason × hold bucket
| bucket | reason | n | sum |
|---|---|---|---|
| sub60 | target_hit | 47 | +$23.64 |
| ge60 | target_hit | 13 | +$2.02 |
| ge60 | timeout (300s) | 8 | −$8.24 |
| ge60 | hard_sl_net | 2 | −$6.85 |

### cut60 impact decomposition on the ≥60s bucket
- **12 late WINNERS (cut60 DAMAGES), +$2.04 at risk** — target_hit at 65-300s. cut60 kills
  them at 60s before they reach target. Several show max_adverse ≫ max_favorable (e.g.
  AIGENSYN adv=1.83%/fav=0.28%, BOBBOB adv=1.60%/fav=0.50%) → these went AGAINST first then
  reverted to target, so at 60s they were likely at a LOSS. cut60 plausibly converts most of
  this +$2.04 to ~0 or slightly negative. (All are small, $0.05-0.39 each.)
- **11 late LOSERS (cut60 HELPS), −$15.11 reducible** — 8 timeouts (−$8.24, exit at 60s = only
  partial adverse drift captured → cut60 saves the post-60s portion) + 2 hard_sl_net (−$6.85;
  BOBBOB −$3.70 hit SL at 90s, AIGENSYN −$3.15 hit SL at 288s → cut60 at 60s exits BEFORE the
  late SL cross on AIGENSYN, but BOBBOB's adverse was fast (adv 3.5% by 90s) so cut60 still
  eats most of it). One "loser" (10000NEX −$0.02 target_hit 105s) is just a fee-loss.

### Honest cut60 total-PnL bounds (sub-60s kept = $23.64 unchanged)
| scenario | total | meaning |
|---|---|---|
| v6 actual | **$10.57** | baseline |
| cut60 WORST | $8.53 | winners→0, losers full (cut60 *hurts*) |
| cut60 MID 50/50 | $17.10 | winners keep half, losers cut half |
| cut60 BEST | $25.68 | winners full, losers→0 (= naive "recover drag") |

**Central plausible ≈ $14-17** (winners→~0, timeout-losers cut ~half, SL-losers cut ~30%).
Net realistic improvement over v6 ≈ **+$3 to +$7 on this 70-trade sample** — directionally
positive, worth running, but materially less than the naive +$13 "drag recovery." This is the
quantified form of the cycle-1700 caveat ("DOWNGRADES expected 60s-cutoff uplift"): cut60 buys
loss-capping at the cost of late-winner sacrifice; the net is modestly positive, dominated by
how much of the 300s-timeout drift accrues after 60s (unobservable → A/B settles it).
