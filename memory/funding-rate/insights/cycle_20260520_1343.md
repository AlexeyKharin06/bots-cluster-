## Conclusions

### What I rejected and WHY
1. **R8 known<5 + smart 3-5 as profitability gate** — looked great in-sample (rug 10%, n=245) but walk-forward shows test rug jumped to 32%. Effect was regime, not feature.
2. **R9 age >= 30 as standalone hypothesis** — n=5 in test is too small to validate. Promising but inconclusive — *don't* mark rejected, mark "needs more data".
3. **R10 single-feature filter for profitability** — no univariate filter is positive in absolute PnL. All filters degrade out of sample due to regime shift. Filters can only reduce loss, not turn it positive.

### What I learned (NEW knowledge for project memory)
1. **Smart/known wallet count is INVERTED**: more smart wallets ≠ better outcome. Sweet spot is 3-5 smart. The current "SMART_COPY" streams (n=339, avg -48%) are buying tokens with too many smart wallets, which is actually a rug pattern (cluster dump).
2. **Big winners ≠ safe filter intersection**: every >200% token had heavy wallet clustering (smart 7-12, known 17-18). Safe filter removes ALL of them. There are two distinct trading strategies: rug-avoidance (safe filter, -23% avg, no lottery) vs lottery-hunting (cluster signal, 60% rug, occasional 30x).
3. **Recent 24h regime is hostile**: avg -59% vs prior -52%. -7pp regime drag. This explains the catastrophic last 100 trades.
4. **Liq_mcap_ratio > 100 = death trap**: 74% rug rate, n=1406. Strongest single negative signal.
5. **age_min < 10 is dangerous, 30+ may be edge**: lt_10 has 51% rug, 30+ has only 21% rug. Bot might benefit from delayed entry rather than instant.
