### 3.7 Mechanism diagnosis: partial reversion on TIMEOUT trades

Per-event TIMEOUT analysis (n=18):
- 72.2% of TIMEOUT trades show ANY peg-ward movement (exit price closer to $1 than entry)
- Median entry deviation: −54bp
- Median exit deviation: −23bp
- Mean absolute reduction in deviation across all TIMEOUT trades: 34bp

In words: even when a stable doesn't FULLY revert to within 10bp of peg in 7 days, on average it closes 60% of the way (54bp → 23bp). That 34bp of partial reversion is what produces the positive TIMEOUT-bucket mean.

Compare to fairprice scalp TIMEOUT mechanism: target is "+0.2% from entry"; if target doesn't print, price has DRIFTED away by ~−1.7% on average (mega_fairprice LONG TIMEOUT mean −1.74%); the drift is random-walk-like and TARGET-hit acts as adverse selection.

**The two mechanisms are structurally different:**
- Fairprice scalp: ARBITRARY directional bet relative to fair-price anchor; TIMEOUT means "small move didn't fire" but drift is dominant force → adverse selection on left tail.
- H3 depeg: REVERT bet to a fixed strong anchor ($1 from mint/redemption arb); TIMEOUT means "didn't FULLY revert in 7d" but anchor is still attractive force → benign partial reversion.

## 7. Risk / what I might be wrong about

- **n=18 TIMEOUT for 50bp tier is small.** A few more TIMEOUT trades in next-3-months forward window could flip the bucket negative. But the cushion is large: TIMEOUT mean +0.338% and 13 of 18 are positive; to push the bucket to mean=0 we'd need ~3 large negative outcomes (−1% each) without offsetting positives. Possible but unlikely given the mechanism.
- **TUSD SHORT TIMEOUT cell (n=5, +0.208%, 60% WR) is borderline.** If we extend forward and this cell drifts to negative, the TUSD SHORT class alone might pass Meth #28's bimodality test. But: cycle 2300's Meth #14 SOLOFLAG filter would drop 3 of those 5 losses (they're CONFIRMED-class multi-venue), making this cell ALL positive. The "TUSD without SOLOFLAG" universe is the only place where Meth #28 could ever apply to H3.
- **The 75bp tier (n=3 TIMEOUT) is too small for any claim.** TIMEOUT mean +1.881% in 3 events could easily flip with one bad TUSD event. But this only strengthens the negative finding; the small-sample TIMEOUT happens to be even MORE positive than TARGET, which is the opposite direction Meth #28 would predict.
- **Best-case zero-PnL assumption in early-cut experiments is too generous.** If we assumed partial-PnL (linear interpolation), the mean might increase slightly because some events would still capture partial reversion. But the WR collapse (96.1% → 64-77%) is the strongest signal that early-cut destroys H3 economics — that part is robust to PnL assumption because it depends on whether the event hits target by the cutoff, not on the PnL value at cutoff.
- **I might be wrong about the boundary's exact dividing line.** "Scalp" vs "mean-rev-to-anchor" is a reasonable framing but might miss a third class (e.g., perp basis arbitrage). The corroboration-#2 candidate suggestion is honest about this.
