## PnL model

For each LONG-only event (n=116, pre_rate<0 subset of 213 shortenings):
```
primary LONG perp:  PnL_funding = -primary_rate × n_periods   (intensified neg on primary)
hedge SHORT perp:   PnL_funding = +hedge_rate (signed, summed over 4h on hedge ex)
price PnL:          basis risk between exchanges, assumed ≤5bp (perp-perp on same sym_norm)
slippage:           4 legs × 5bp = 20bp = 0.0020

net_h34 = primary_fund_cum4 (already trader-sign-adjusted in h31_net) + hedge_fund_cum4h_raw - SLIP
```

## Hedge-exchange selection rules (decision uses only info ≤ event_ts)

A. **hedge_pre_max_pos**: hedge ex with most-positive pre_rate (smallest funding cost for SHORT)
B. **hedge_pre_min_abs**: hedge ex with smallest |pre_rate|
C. **fixed_binance_else_bybit**: deterministic — binance if primary≠binance, else bybit
D. **oracle_post_hoc**: hedge ex with max realized hedge_fund_cum4h (UPPER BOUND, not honest)

## Why H34 is ~half the edge of basis-hedged

When one exchange shortens its funding interval (the H31 trigger), it's typically because that
exchange is reacting to a deep funding dislocation that is being expressed on all major venues.
Other exchanges trading the same `sym_norm` therefore also have intensified-negative funding
during the post-shortening window (high cross-exchange funding correlation). A SHORT-perp leg
on a hedge exchange therefore *eats roughly half* of the primary's intensified gain — the basis
hedge (SHORT-spot, no funding charge) does not.

This is structural, not noise. Expected H34/H31 edge ratio ≈ 0.4× is a feature of the regime,
not something selection can fix beyond a few bp.

## Caveats

1. **Price PnL on perp-perp hedge** is assumed ≤5bp (rolled into the 4-leg slippage). On same
   `sym_norm` across two perp venues, prices typically track within a few bp; meaningful basis
   risk only appears on micro-caps with thin order books. H36 will quantify this for the
   smallest 30 symbols.

2. **Hedge funding cum** is computed as raw sum of funding events landing in (ts, ts+4h] on the
   hedge ex. Different hedge ex's may have funding at 1h/4h/8h intervals, so the count of
   funding events varies (0-4 within 4h). The economic substance is correct — if hedge ex
   charges funding only once in the 4h window, that one charge is the entire hedge PnL.

3. **15 uncovered events** are a structural limit: if the symbol isn't listed on a second
   perp venue, no cross-exchange hedge exists. Routing to `skip` is the honest answer.

4. **Rule A is not robust to the rare case** where hedge ex's pre-rate is positive but post
   becomes very negative (hedge funding "follows" primary post-event). The data show this
   happens but isn't dominant — Sharpe 0.82 with min event −3.43%. A tighter rule could
   require hedge_pre_rate > +30bp threshold, but n shrinks and gains may not justify the
   selection.

5. **Sample remains modest** at n=101 with hedge available. Confidence will rise as the
   180-day parquet rolls forward.
