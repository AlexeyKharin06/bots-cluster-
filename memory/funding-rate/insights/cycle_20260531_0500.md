### Permutation null on extended tier
Implementation: sign-flip permutation (multiply each `real_pnl` by random ±1). 5000 perms, seed=7.
- obs mean = +2.875%
- null mean = −0.008% (centred on 0, as expected)
- null std = 0.326%
- z-score: 2.875 / 0.326 ≈ **8.8σ**
- p(|null| ≥ |obs|) = 0.00000 (0 of 5000)

Bootstrap (2000 resamples): 95% CI [+2.327%, +3.528%], P(mean>30bp) = 1.000.

This is the **first permutation null computed on the 4-ex pool** (cycle 0500 did binance solo, n=564, with a non-sign-flip variant). It corroborates Edge 1.2 (C2_p99_4EX_QUALITY) as a real edge, not a multiple-testing artefact.

## Findings summary

### NEW operational principle (worth promotion to Methodology #34 candidate)
**"Strategy-availability gate": before adding a symbol to a multi-venue strategy, verify the strategy is OPERATIONALLY EXECUTABLE on each venue (all required legs listed). Triggers on venues where one leg is missing are non-actionable and must not enter the pooled stats.** Demonstrated by TAC: 8 binance "triggers" in `c2_wide` are not strategy-executable because binance has no TAC spot. The c2 fetcher already filters this correctly via `no_spot_kline` err — promote the principle.

This is structurally distinct from Meth #33 (per-venue Sharpe gate). Meth #33 says: "even when both legs ARE listed, reject the venue if per-venue Sharpe < pool Sharpe." Meth #34-candidate says: "before even reaching Meth #33, gate on dual-leg availability." It's a precondition.

### NEW blacklist additions (extended quality tier)
- **MOVR@bybit, MOVR@gate** (n=5 total, mean −0.36% pooled). Cross-venue adverse pattern, both venues negative. Mechanism unknown — could be venue-specific perp/spot index divergence OR symbol-wide adverse drift.
- **INJ@binance, INJ@gate** (n=2 total, both negative). Tiny sample; very weak evidence. Recommended add to "watch" list, not hard blacklist.

### CORROBORATION
- **TAC** structurally adverse (cycle 2300's hypothesis confirmed for the venues where the test can be run; unfalsifiable on binance/gate/okx due to leg availability).
- **Permutation null** for 4-ex pool: edge is ~9σ significant, p<<0.001.
- **Paper bots** healthy and running; low signal-rate is universe-driven, not process-driven.
