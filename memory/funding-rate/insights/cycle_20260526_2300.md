## 7. HEDGE-RATIO SCALER FORM — REJECTED

For completeness tested the continuous-h interpolation:

`net(h) = (1-h)*net_unhedged + h*net_basis`

Two scaler hypotheses:
- `rank_to_hedge`: h = (rank-1)/(n_total-1) — high rank (least extreme on primary) → high hedge
- `inv_rank_to_hedge`: h = 1 - (rank-1)/(n_total-1) — low rank (most extreme on primary) → high hedge

Result:
- rank_to_hedge: mean +4.42% / WR **65.5%** / Sh +0.22 (cannot accept WR drop)
- inv_rank_to_hedge: mean +3.28% / WR **87.1%** / Sh +0.35 (still drops WR)

Mechanism: any reduction below h=1.0 re-introduces unhedged price-direction variance. H31_BASIS's structural 100% WR comes from min |funding|>>basis cost — backing off the hedge loses that property. **The right place to apply rank/n_neg signal is FILTERING which events to take, NOT scaling how much to hedge them.** Rejected the scaler form; FILTER form is the operational mechanism.
