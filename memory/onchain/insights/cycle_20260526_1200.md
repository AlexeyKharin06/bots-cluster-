## Step 5 — Methodology Lesson #20 candidate: NEAR-BIG FEATURE-DISTRIBUTION TRAP

**Lesson**: Pattern-matching outcome-selected cohorts (top N% by PnL) to derive a filter has a selection-bias trap. The features of "winners" overlap heavily with the features of "losers" in the same base population. Median-of-winners is NOT a discriminator unless validated AGAINST the full population.

**Implication for past brain reasoning**:
- **Methodology #17 (NEAR-BIG REGIME, c1200)** must be REVISED. Near-bigs are NOT a "shape-similar weaker-magnitude cohort" — they're survivor noise.

- The TRUE alpha is **wallet identity (HUPHey)** + **specific cohort wallets (0x85871, 75qsE3p5y2BF)** — these are NAMED entities, not feature-distribution shapes.
