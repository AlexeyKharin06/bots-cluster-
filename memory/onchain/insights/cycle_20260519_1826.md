## Headline result — NULL_TG_LEAD

**Of 584 sniper trade tokens, only 3 overlap with the 8206 TG-signal-mentioned tokens. Of those 3, ZERO have a TG mention before sniper entry.**

| sniper token (sym) | sniper pnl | gap entry→first_mention | mentioning channel |
|---|---|---|---|
| GG9UhZ.. (Staring) | -18.7% | +604 min | memecrypted_chat |
| 5GEuk2.. (STX)      | -100%  | +209 min | id_3076702300 |
| 3iE8Tu.. (MEMERIES) | -88.2% | +151 min | memecrypted_chat |

**Mechanism**: sniper trades very-early-stage low-liquidity tokens (entry typically <5 min after pair creation). TG channels in our corpus mention tokens **151-604 min post-entry** — they react to flow, they don't lead it. The corpus is dominated by `memecrypted_chat` (10134 mentions), `DCATrack` (6891), `lexch4t` (6201) — all reactive aggregators, not signal-generators.

This is a clean **null result** for the H_TG_LEAD family of hypotheses as currently formulated. No paper-stream candidate exists — the joint distribution of (trade tokens × channels with prior signal) is empty.
