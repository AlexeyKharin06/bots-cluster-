### **NEW Methodology Lesson #9 (proposed): STATE-WINDOW ROTATION BIAS**

**Symptom**: TG-2h stats appear to weaken cross-cycle (c0600 K=0.145 → c1200 K=0.127) but a non-trivial portion of the weakening is **mechanical**, due to MEMEWC + PEDUCK aging out of state.json's rolling ~3-day window. Those C3 PORTUGAL bigs (+179, +908) were anchoring TEST stats.

**Root cause**: `state.json` is a rolling window of recent activity, NOT a permanent record. FIXED boundary methodology (lesson #7) anchors the TEST.first timestamp, but **the source data itself shrinks from the left** as old entries are purged.

**Implication**:
- Cross-cycle persistence comparisons need a "rotation-adjusted" caveat
